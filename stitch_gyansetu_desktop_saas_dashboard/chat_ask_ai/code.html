Based on the localized knowledge pack for the Eastern District (v.2.4), the eligibility criteria for the Marginal Farmer Agricultural Subsidy are as follows:

  • Land Holding: Must own and cultivate less than 2 hectares (approx. 5 acres) of land.
  • Residency: Must be a registered resident of the designated eastern district for at least 5 consecutive years.
  • Crop Type: The subsidy primarily targets farmers cultivating drought-resistant crops or engaging in approved multi-cropping practices.
  • Income Threshold: Total annual household income from all sources must not exceed ₹1,50,000.

Applicants must provide a valid Farmer ID and recent land records to the local agricultural extension office during the application window.
