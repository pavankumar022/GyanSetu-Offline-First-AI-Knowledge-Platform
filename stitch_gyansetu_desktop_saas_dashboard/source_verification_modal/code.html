Rural Education Policy 2023

account_balance Dept of Education calendar_today Oct 12, 2023

Document Excerpt

The comprehensive policy framework outlines strategic interventions aimed at bridging the digital divide in rural educational institutions. Key initiatives include the deployment of offline-first digital resource centers, ensuring uninterrupted access to critical curriculum materials regardless of internet connectivity. Funding allocations prioritize structural improvements, teacher training programs focused on blended learning methodologies, and the procurement of resilient hardware capable of withstanding varied environmental conditions. The policy mandates bi-annual audits to measure the efficacy of these implementations against predefined educational outcomes. Furthermore, community engagement models are established to foster local ownership and maintenance of educational infrastructure, integrating local stakeholders into the decision-making processes regarding resource utilization and curriculum contextualization.
