Child Malnutrition Screening, Anemia Mukt Bharat & POSHAN Abhiyaan Protocol
Source: Ministry of Women and Child Development (MoWCD); Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW) & ICDS

1. Child Malnutrition Assessment & MUAC Tape Protocol:
• Severe Acute Malnutrition (SAM):
  - Mid-Upper Arm Circumference (MUAC) < 11.5 cm (Red zone on MUAC tape) or presence of bilateral pitting edema.
  - Weight-for-Height Z-score < -3 SD.
  - Action: Immediate referral to Nutrition Rehabilitation Centre (NRC) at District Hospital / CHC for therapeutic feeding (F-75 / F-100 milk).
• Moderate Acute Malnutrition (MAM):
  - MUAC 11.5 cm to 12.5 cm (Yellow zone).
  - Action: Anganwadi double fortification energy-dense take-home ration (THR) + POSHAN Abhiyaan counseling.

2. Anemia Mukt Bharat (AMB) Iron Supplementation Protocol:
• Children 6 to 59 Months:
  - 1 ml Iron-Folic Acid (IFA) Syrup twice a week (containing 20 mg elemental Iron + 100 mcg Folic Acid).
• Children 5 to 9 Years:
  - 1 Pink IFA Tablet weekly (45 mg elemental Iron + 400 mcg Folic Acid).
• Adolescents 10 to 19 Years:
  - 1 Blue IFA Tablet weekly (60 mg elemental Iron + 500 mcg Folic Acid) distributed at schools and Anganwadi centers.
• Deworming Protocol (Bi-Annual National Deworming Day - NDD):
  - Albendazole 400 mg single tablet (chewable) or 200 mg (half tablet for children 1-2 years) given every 6 months (February & August).

3. Infant and Young Child Feeding (IYCF) Guidelines:
• Early Initiation of Breastfeeding: Within 1 hour of birth (colostrum first milk is essential immunity booster).
• Exclusive Breastfeeding: For first 6 months (no water, honey, or top milk).
• Complementary Feeding: Introduce soft thick porridge/mashed food at 6 months alongside continued breastfeeding up to 2 years.
