ASHA Worker Guidelines — Maternal Health, ANC Checkups & High-Risk Pregnancy SOP
Source: National Health Mission (NHM), Ministry of Health and Family Welfare; ASHA Training Modules

Mandatory Antenatal Care (ANC) Visits & Schedule:
• Minimum 4 Mandatory ANC Visits for every pregnant woman:
  1. 1st ANC Visit: Within 12 weeks of pregnancy (first trimester — early registration). Confirm pregnancy with Nishchay kit, issue MCP Card (Mother & Child Protection Card).
  2. 2nd ANC Visit: Between 14 and 26 weeks (second trimester).
  3. 3rd ANC Visit: Between 28 and 34 weeks (third trimester).
  4. 4th ANC Visit: Between 36 weeks and delivery.

Essential Clinical Tests at Every ANC Visit:
• Weight measurement (expected weight gain 10–12 kg during pregnancy).
• Blood Pressure (BP) checking (detect pre-eclampsia / hypertension if BP > 140/90 mmHg).
• Hemoglobin (Hb) testing (detect anemia if Hb < 11 g/dL).
• Abdominal palpation (fundal height, fetal heart rate FHR after 20 weeks).
• Urine test for albumin (proteinuria) and sugar.

Maternal Nutrition & Supplementation Protocol:
• Iron & Folic Acid (IFA) Tablets: 1 red IFA tablet daily (100 mg elemental Iron + 500 mcg Folic Acid) starting from 4th month for 180 days. Double dose (2 tablets daily) if Hb < 11 g/dL (anemia).
• Folic Acid: 1 tablet (5 mg) daily during first 3 months of pregnancy to prevent neural tube defects.
• Calcium & Vitamin D3: 2 Calcium tablets (500 mg elemental Calcium + 250 IU Vit D3) daily from 14th week onwards for 180 days (never take Calcium and IFA tablets simultaneously — take 4 hours apart).
• Tetanus Toxoid / Td Injections: Td-1 dose as early as possible in pregnancy; Td-2 dose 4 weeks after Td-1.

High-Risk Pregnancy (HRP) Danger Signs — Immediate PHC / CHC Referral:
1. Severe anemia (Hb < 7 g/dL) or extreme pallor.
2. Severe headache, blurred vision, swelling of face/hands (signs of Pre-Eclampsia / Eclampsia).
3. Vaginal bleeding or spotty discharge at any stage of pregnancy.
4. High fever with chills (suspected malaria/pyelonephritis).
5. High blood pressure (BP >= 140/90 mmHg).
6. Convulsions / fits (Eclampsia emergency — administer Inj. Magnesium Sulfate 5g IM per buttock before transport).
7. Premature labor pains or rupture of membranes (leaking water before 37 weeks).
8. Absence of fetal movements after 24 weeks.

Institutional Delivery Incentives (Janani Suraksha Yojana - JSY):
• JSY Cash Incentive for Rural Institutional Delivery: ₹1,400 to pregnant mother + ₹600 to ASHA worker for accompanying patient.
• Call 108 / 102 National Ambulance Service for free transport to FRU (First Referral Unit / CHC).
