Rural First Aid Guidelines — Snake Bite, Heat Stroke, Burns & Fractures SOP
Source: Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW); Indian National Snakebite Protocol & First Aid Manual

1. Snake Bite Management Protocol (RIGHT Protocol):
• DO NOT: Never cut the bite wound, never suck venom, never apply tight cord/tourniquet, never apply ice/herbal paste, never give alcohol/painkillers (aspirin).
• DO (RIGHT Protocol):
  - R = Reassure victim (panic accelerates venom absorption via tachycardia).
  - I = Immobilize bitten limb using splint or sling at or below heart level (do not move bitten limb).
  - G = Get to hospital immediately (call 108 Emergency Ambulance for Polyvalent Anti-Snake Venom - ASV administration).
  - H = Tell Hospital staff about symptoms (ptosis / drooping eyelids, respiratory distress, local swelling/bleeding).
  - T = Tell Time of bite and monitor vitals during transport.

2. Heat Stroke & Heat Exhaustion Protocol:
• Symptoms: High body temperature (>40°C / 104°F), dry hot skin (no sweating), altered mental state / confusion, rapid pulse, dizziness.
• First Aid Actions:
  - Immediately move patient to cool shade or ventilated room.
  - Remove excess clothing. Spray or sponge body with cold water and fan vigorously.
  - Apply ice packs / wet cold cloths to neck, armpits, and groin areas.
  - If conscious: Give Oral Rehydration Solution (ORS) or cool water with salt/sugar.
  - DO NOT give aspirin or paracetamol for heat stroke (worsens liver/kidney injury). Rush to PHC/CHC.

3. First Aid for Burns (Thermal & Chemical):
• First-Degree & Second-Degree Burns (Red skin, blisters):
  - Immediately pour clean running cool water over burn area for 15–20 minutes.
  - Cover with clean sterile non-stick cloth or gauze.
  - DO NOT pop blisters, do not apply ghee, ink, toothpaste, or ice.
  - Give ORS to prevent burn shock dehydration.
• Third-Degree Burns (Charred/white skin): Do not pour water. Cover with clean dry cloth, elevate burned limbs, and transport to Tertiary Hospital immediately.

4. Fractures & Severe Wound First Aid:
• Fractures: Immobilize injured bone above and below fracture joint using rigid wooden splint, folded newspaper, or cardboard tied with cloth strips. Do not try to realign broken bones.
• Severe Bleeding: Apply firm direct pressure over wound using clean sterile cloth for 10–15 minutes. Elevate bleeding limb above heart level. Use tourniquet ONLY for life-threatening arterial limb bleeding 5 cm above wound.
