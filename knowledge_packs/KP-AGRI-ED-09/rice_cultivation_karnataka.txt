Rice (Paddy) Cultivation Guidelines — Karnataka Advisory
Source: University of Agricultural Sciences (UAS), Mandya & Shivamogga; ICAR-NRRI Cuttack

Suitable Agro-Climatic Zones for Rice (Paddy) in Karnataka:
• Key Zones: Zone 9 (Southern Transition Zone), Zone 10 (Coastal Zone), Zone 6 (Southern Dry Zone), and Canal Command Areas of Zone 3 (Northern Dry Zone).
• Major Rice Districts: Mandya (Paddy heartland of Old Mysore), Shimoga, Davanagere, Mysuru, Hassan, Udupi, Dakshina Kannada, Uttara Kannada, Raichur, and Koppal (Tungabhadra left bank canal belt).

Recommended Rice (Paddy) Varieties for Karnataka:
• Irrigated Canal Command (Mandya, Mysuru, Davanagere):
  - KMP-175, Tanu (KMP-101), Jaya, Jyothi, BPT-5204 (Sona Masuri), RNR-15048 (Telangana Sona - low GI rice), MTU-1010. Yield: 55–70 q/ha.
• Coastal High Rainfall Zone (Udupi, Mangaluru, Karwar):
  - MO-4, Intan, Phalguna, Panchami, Sahyadri Hybrids (Sahyadri-3). Flood and submergence tolerant. Yield: 45–55 q/ha.
• Rainfed Uplands / Malnad:
  - Abhilash, Tunga, KHP-2, Sharavathi. Yield: 35–45 q/ha.

Sowing & Transplanting Window for Rice (Paddy):
• Kharif Season: Nursery sowing June 1 to June 30; Transplanting 21–25 days old nursery seedlings from June 25 to July 25. Seed rate: 25 kg/ha for transplanted paddy.
• Summer Rice (Rabi/Summer): Nursery sowing Dec 15 to Jan 15; Transplanting Jan 10 to Feb 10.

Soil & Land Preparation for Rice (Paddy):
• Soil Type: Clay loams, silty clay, and heavy alluvial soils with pH 5.5–7.0.
• Puddling & Land Prep: 2–3 wet ploughings in standing water followed by leveling using a wooden plank to minimize percolation water loss. Maintain 5 cm water depth during transplanting.

Fertilizer Recommendations for Rice (Paddy) in Karnataka (NPK per Hectare):
• Irrigated Transplanted Paddy: 100 kg Nitrogen (N) : 50 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 50 kg Potassium (K2O).
  - Basal Dose: 50% Nitrogen + 100% Phosphorus + 50% Potassium at transplanting.
  - Active Tillering Top-Dressing (25–30 Days After Transplanting - DAT): 25% Nitrogen.
  - Panicle Initiation Top-Dressing (45–50 DAT): 25% Nitrogen + 50% Potassium.
  - Zinc Sulfate: Apply Zinc Sulfate @ 20 kg/ha basal to treat Khaira disease (zinc deficiency).
• Rainfed / Coastal Rice: 75 kg Nitrogen : 37.5 kg Phosphorus : 37.5 kg Potassium per hectare.

Critical Irrigation & Growth Stages for Rice (Paddy):
1. Nursery Stage: 0–25 days in nursery bed.
2. Transplanting & Seedling Establishment: 0–10 DAT (maintain 2 cm standing water).
3. Active Tillering Stage: 25–35 DAT (critical for tiller count).
4. Panicle Initiation (PI): 45–50 DAT (MOST CRITICAL moisture stage — water deficit cuts yield by 50%).
5. Flowering & Heading Stage: 65–70 DAT.
6. Grain Filling & Dough Stage: 85–90 DAT.
7. Water Drainage: Drain field completely 10–12 days prior to harvest.

Pest & Disease Management for Rice (Paddy):
• Paddy Blast (Magnaporthe oryzae): Spray Tricyclazole 75 WP @ 0.6 g/litre or Isoprothiolane 40 EC @ 1.5 ml/litre at neck blast emergence.
• Brown Plant Hopper (BPH - Nilaparvata lugens): Spray Pymetrozine 50 WG @ 0.6 g/litre or Triflumezopyrim 10 SC @ 0.5 ml/litre at base of hills when BPH count >10 per hill.
• Paddy Stem Borer (Scirpophaga incertulas): Apply Chlorantraniliprole 0.4% GR @ 4 kg/acre or Cartap hydrochloride 4G @ 7.5 kg/acre at 20 DAT.
