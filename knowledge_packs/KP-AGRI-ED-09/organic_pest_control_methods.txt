Organic & Non-Chemical Pest Control Methods Guidelines
Source: ICAR-National Centre for Integrated Pest Management (NCIPM); NCOF Ghaziabad & National Mission on Sustainable Agriculture (NMSA)

1. Botanical Formulations & Bio-Pesticides:
• Neem Kernel Aqueous Extract (NSKE 5%): Grind 50 kg neem seed kernels, soak in 100 L water overnight with 200 g soap powder. Filter and dilute to 1000 L/ha. Effective against sucking pests, caterpillars, and leaf miners.
• Neem Oil 10,000 ppm (EC formulation): Spray @ 2–3 ml/litre water + 1 ml sticker. Acts as oviposition deterrent, anti-feedant, and insect growth regulator.
• Agniastra (Organic Pest Repellent): Boil 10 kg neem leaves + 2 kg tobacco leaves + 2 kg hot green chillies + 2 kg garlic paste in 200 L cow urine. Dilute 6 L Agniastra in 200 L water/acre for bollworms and caterpillars.
• Brahmastra: Decoction of neem, custard apple, papaya, pomegranate, and guava leaves in cow urine. Effective against sucking pests.

2. Bio-Dynamic Liquid Formulations (Natural Farming):
• Jeevamrutha: Mix 10 kg fresh cow dung + 10 L cow urine + 2 kg jaggery + 2 kg pulse flour + 1 handful fertile soil in 200 L water. Ferment for 48 hours. Apply 200 L/acre through irrigation water every 15 days to boost soil micro-flora.
• Panchagavya: Mix cow dung, cow urine, milk, curd, ghee, tender coconut water, banana, and sugarcane juice. Spray 3% solution (30 ml/litre) at 15-day intervals as growth promoter and immunity booster.

3. Physical & Mechanical Trap Controls:
• Yellow Sticky Traps: Install 15–20 yellow sticky traps/acre at crop canopy level to catch whiteflies, aphids, thrips, and leaf miners.
• Blue Sticky Traps: Install 15 traps/acre specifically for thrips control in chilli, onion, and cotton.
• Pheromone Traps: Install 5–8 pheromone traps/acre with species-specific lures (Helilure for Helicoverpa, Spodolure for Spodoptera, Pectinolure for Pink Bollworm).
• Light Traps: Set up solar light traps @ 1 trap/ha to capture nocturnal adult moths and beetles.

4. Biological Control Agents (Bio-Agents):
• Trichogramma chilonis (Egg Parasitoid): Release Trichocards @ 5 cards/ha (1,00,000 parasitized eggs/ha) at weekly intervals for sugarcane stem borer and cotton bollworms.
• Chrysoperla carnea (Green Lacewing): Release 10,000 larvae/ha against aphids, whiteflies, and mealybugs.
• Pseudomonas fluorescens & Trichoderma viride: Soil application @ 2.5 kg/ha mixed with 100 kg FYM for root rot and wilt management.
