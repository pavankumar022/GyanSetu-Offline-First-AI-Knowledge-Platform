Green Gram (Moong), Black Gram (Urad) & Lentil (Masoor) Cultivation Guidelines
Source: ICAR-Indian Institute of Pulses Research (IIPR), Kanpur; UAS Dharwad & TNAU

Suitable Agro-Climatic Zones & Soil Requirements:
• Zones & Regions: Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Karnataka (Northern Dry Zone & Rice Fallows of Coastal/Malnad), Andhra Pradesh, and Bihar.
• Soil & pH: Well-drained fertile loams to clay loams with pH 6.5–7.5. Highly sensitive to waterlogging.

Recommended Varieties by Season:
• Moong (Green Gram):
  - Kharif / Summer: IPM 02-3 (Meha), HUM-16, Shiny Moong, DGG-5 (for Karnataka). Yield: 12–15 q/ha.
• Urad (Black Gram):
  - Kharif / Rabi Rice Fallow: LBG-752, LBG-623, AKU-15, DU-1 (for South India). Yield: 10–14 q/ha.
• Masoor (Lentil):
  - Rabi Season: L-4076, HUL-57, DPL-62, K-75. Yield: 15–20 q/ha.

Sowing Seasons & Planting Window:
• Kharif Season: June 15 to July 15 (with arrival of monsoon rains).
• Rabi / Rice Fallows: October 15 to November 15 (sowing directly into standing rice stubble moist soil).
• Summer (Zaid) Season: March 1 to March 25 (under assured irrigation after wheat/mustard harvest).

Row Spacing & Seed Rate:
• Moong / Urad Seed Rate: Kharif: 15–20 kg/ha (30 cm x 10 cm spacing). Summer / Rice Fallows: 25 kg/ha (22.5 cm x 10 cm spacing).
• Lentil (Masoor) Seed Rate: 40–45 kg/ha (22.5 cm x 5 cm spacing).
• Seed Treatment: Rhizobium leguminosarum @ 5 g/kg seed + PSB @ 5 g/kg seed + Trichoderma viride @ 5 g/kg seed.

Irrigation Requirement & Water Management:
• Water Type: Rainfed for Kharif & Rice Fallows; Irrigated for Summer (Zaid).
• Critical Growth Stages needing Irrigation:
  - Summer Moong/Urad: Requires 3–4 light irrigations at 10–12 day intervals.
  - Critical Stages: Flowering (30–35 DAS) and Pod Development (45–50 DAS). Avoid excessive irrigation during peak flowering.

Fertilizer & NPK Recommendations (per Hectare):
• Rainfed Kharif Pulse Dose: 20 kg Nitrogen (N) : 40 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 20 kg Potassium (K2O) + 20 kg Sulfur per hectare.
• Basal Application: Apply 100% N, P, K, and S at sowing time. Pulse root nodules fix atmospheric nitrogen.

Growth Stages & Duration:
1. Germination & Seedling Phase: 0–12 Days After Sowing (DAS).
2. Vegetative Branching: 15–30 DAS.
3. Flowering & Pod Initiation: 35–45 DAS.
4. Pod Maturity & Harvesting: 65–75 DAS for Moong/Urad; 110–120 DAS for Lentil.

Common Pest Control (Insect Pests):
• Whitefly & Aphids (Yellow Mosaic Virus vectors): Spray Imidacloprid 17.8 SL @ 0.3 ml/litre or Dimethoate 30 EC @ 1.7 ml/litre.
• Pod Borer (Helicoverpa / Maruca testulalis): Spray Emamectin Benzoate 5 SG @ 0.4 g/litre or Indoxacarb 14.5 SC @ 0.5 ml/litre at 50% flowering.

Common Disease Management (Fungal & Viral):
• Yellow Mosaic Virus (YMV): Grow YMV-resistant varieties (IPM 02-3, DGG-5); control whitefly vector early with yellow sticky traps @ 15/acre.
• Powdery Mildew & Cercospora Leaf Spot: Spray Soluble Sulfur @ 3 g/litre or Carbendazim 50 WP @ 1 g/litre water.

Expected Yield:
• Rainfed Kharif: 10–12 quintals/ha. Irrigated Summer: 14–16 quintals/ha.

Top Producing States:
• Madhya Pradesh (No. 1 pulse producer), Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Maharashtra, Karnataka.
