Horticulture Fruit Crops Cultivation — Mango, Papaya, Grapes, Pomegranate & Citrus
Source: ICAR-Indian Institute of Horticultural Research (IIHR), Bengaluru; ICAR-CISH Lucknow & ICAR-NRCP Solapur

Suitable Agro-Climatic Zones & Soil Requirements:
• Mango: UP (Dasheri/Langra), Maharashtra (Alphonso Ratnagiri), Andhra Pradesh (Banganapalli), Karnataka (Raspuri, Badami, Mallika, Totapuri). Well-drained deep alluvial/red loamy soils with pH 5.5–7.5.
• Papaya: MP, Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh. Rich well-drained sandy loam with pH 6.5–7.0; highly sensitive to waterlogging (stem rot).
• Grapes: Maharashtra (Nashik — "Grape Capital"), Karnataka (Vijayapura, Chikkaballapur, Bengaluru Blue), AP. Well-drained sandy to clay loams with pH 6.5–8.0.
• Pomegranate: Maharashtra (Solapur, Nashik), Karnataka (Koppal, Vijayapura, Bagalkot), Gujarat. Deep loamy Vertisols to light gravelly soils with pH 6.5–8.2.
• Citrus (Sweet Lime / Mosambi & Acid Lime / Lemon): Maharashtra (Nagpur mandarin hub), Andhra Pradesh, Karnataka (Vijayapura kagzi lime), Punjab (Kinnow). Medium black or light loamy soils with pH 6.5–7.5.

Recommended Varieties by Crop:
• Mango: Alphonso (Hapus), Banganapalli, Dasheri, Langra, Totapuri, Kesar, Amrapali (high density), Mallika. Yield: 10–15 tonnes/ha.
• Papaya: Red Lady 786 (gynodioecious commercial hybrid), Arka Surya, Arka Prabhath, Pusa Delicious. Yield: 80–100 tonnes/ha.
• Grapes: Thompson Seedless, Manik Chaman, Sharad Seedless, Bengaluru Blue (GI Tag), Anab-e-Shahi. Yield: 25–30 tonnes/ha.
• Pomegranate: Bhagwa (Kesar — dark red arils), Ganesh, Arakta. Yield: 15–20 tonnes/ha.
• Citrus: Nagpur Mandarin, Mosambi, Kagzi Lime, Kinnow Mandarin. Yield: 15–22 tonnes/ha.

Sowing Seasons & Planting Window:
• Mango & Citrus Planting: June to August (monsoon onset) or October.
• Papaya Planting: February–March or June–July (transplant 45-day nursery seedlings).
• Grapes Planting & Pruning: Planting Oct–Jan; Foundation Pruning April, Fruit Pruning October.
• Pomegranate Planting: June–July (Bahar treatment: Ambe Bahar Jan–Feb, Hasta Bahar Sept–Oct).

Row Spacing & Plant Density:
• Mango Spacing: Normal 10 m x 10 m (100 plants/ha); High Density 5 m x 5 m (400 plants/ha); Amrapali ultra-HD 2.5 m x 2.5 m (1600 plants/ha).
• Papaya Spacing: 1.8 m x 1.8 m (3,000 plants/ha). Pit size: 60 cm x 60 cm x 60 cm.
• Grapes Spacing: Y-trellis 3.0 m x 1.5 m (2,222 vines/ha).
• Pomegranate Spacing: 4.5 m x 3.0 m (740 plants/ha).
• Citrus Spacing: 6.0 m x 6.0 m (277 trees/ha).

Irrigation Requirement & Water Management:
• Water Type: Drip Irrigation highly recommended for all fruit orchards.
• Watering Schedule:
  - Papaya: Drip 15–20 litres/plant/day; avoid flooding trunk base.
  - Grapes: Drip 20–30 litres/vine/day; withhold water 15 days before harvest for sugar TSS (°Brix > 18).
  - Pomegranate: Drip 25–35 litres/tree/day; stress 30 days prior to Bahar regulation to induce flowering.
  - Mango: Water young trees weekly; bearing trees require stress 2 months prior to flowering (Nov–Dec).

Fertilizer & NPK Recommendations (per Tree / per Hectare / Year):
• Mango (Bearing Tree > 10 yrs): 1,000 g N : 500 g P2O5 : 1,000 g K2O + 50 kg FYM per tree per year.
• Papaya (per Plant): 250 g N : 250 g P2O5 : 500 g K2O split in 6 bi-monthly doses.
• Grapes (per Hectare): 500 kg N : 300 kg P2O5 : 800 kg K2O via fertigation.
• Pomegranate (per Tree): 625 g N : 250 g P2O5 : 500 g K2O + 20 kg FYM.
• Citrus (per Tree): 600 g N : 200 g P2O5 : 300 g K2O + Micronutrient zinc/iron spray.

Growth Stages & Harvesting Duration:
• Mango: Flowering Jan–Feb; Harvest April–June (110–120 days from fruit set).
• Papaya: Flowering 4 months after planting; Harvest 9–10 months (continues for 2 years).
• Grapes: Fruit Pruning October; Harvest March–April (135–150 days).
• Pomegranate: Fruit development 150–180 days after Bahar flowering.
• Citrus: Harvest 8–9 months after flowering.

Common Pest Control (Insect Pests):
• Mango Hopper & Mealybug: Spray Imidacloprid 17.8 SL @ 0.3 ml/litre or Buprofezin 25 SC @ 1.5 ml/litre during panicle emergence.
• Papaya Mealybug (Paracoccus marginatus): Release Acerophagus papayae parasitoids @ 100/village; spray Neem oil 10,000 ppm @ 3 ml/litre.
• Grape Thrips & Flea Beetle: Spray Spinetoram 11.7 SC @ 0.8 ml/litre.
• Pomegranate Fruit Borer (Deudorix isocrates): Bag developing fruits with butter paper bags; spray Spinosad 45 SC @ 0.3 ml/litre.
• Citrus Leaf Miner & Fruit Sucking Moth: Spray Spray Imidacloprid @ 0.3 ml/litre.

Common Disease Management (Fungal & Bacterial):
• Mango Anthracnose & Powdery Mildew: Spray Azoxystrobin 23 SC @ 1 ml/litre or Hexaconazole 5 EC @ 1 ml/litre.
• Papaya Stem Rot / Foot Rot (Pythium aphanidermatum): Soil drenching with Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2 g/litre.
• Grape Downy Mildew & Powdery Mildew: Spray Cymoxanil + Mancozeb @ 2 g/litre; spray Myclobutanil 10 WP @ 0.4 g/litre.
• Pomegranate Bacterial Blight (Teliya / Xanthomonas axonopodis pv. punicae): Spray Streptocycline @ 0.5 g/litre + Copper Oxychloride @ 2 g/litre at 10-day intervals.
• Citrus Canker (Xanthomonas): Spray Copper Oxychloride @ 3 g/litre + Streptocycline @ 0.5 g/litre.

Expected Yield:
• Mango: 10–15 t/ha. Papaya: 80–100 t/ha. Grapes: 25–30 t/ha. Pomegranate: 15–20 t/ha. Citrus: 15–22 t/ha.

Top Producing States:
• Mango: UP, AP, MH, KA. Papaya: MP, Gujarat. Grapes: MH (Nashik). Pomegranate: MH (Solapur), KA. Citrus: MH (Nagpur), AP.
