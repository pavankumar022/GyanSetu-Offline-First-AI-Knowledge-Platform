Sunflower Cultivation Guidelines — National & Karnataka Advisory
Source: ICAR-Indian Institute of Oilseeds Research (IIOR), Hyderabad; UAS Bangalore & UAS Dharwad (Oilseeds Scheme)

Suitable Agro-Climatic Zones for Sunflower:
• Top Producing States: Karnataka (No. 1 sunflower producing state in India, contributing >50% of national production), Odisha, Maharashtra, Telangana, Bihar, Andhra Pradesh, and Punjab.
• Karnataka Sunflower Belts: Vijayapura, Bagalkot, Koppal, Raichur, Bellary, Dharwad, Belagavi, Chitradurga, and Gadag.

Recommended Sunflower Hybrids & Varieties:
• High Yielding Hybrids: KBSH-41, KBSH-44, KBSH-53, DRSF-108, Sunbred-275, PAC-36, RSFH-130, DRSH-1 (hybrids developed by UAS Bangalore & IIOR). Yield: 20–28 q/ha seed with 40–44% oil content.
• Varieties / Short Duration: Morden (short duration 80–85 days suitable for late sowing or intercropping). Yield: 12–16 q/ha.

Sowing Seasons & Seed Rate for Sunflower:
• Kharif Season: July 1 to August 15. Seed rate: 5 kg hybrid seed/ha (60 cm x 30 cm spacing).
• Rabi / Winter Season: October 15 to November 30.
• Summer Season (Irrigated): January 15 to February 28.

Soil & Land Preparation for Sunflower:
• Soil Type: Well-drained medium to deep black clay soils (Vertisols) or alluvial loams with pH 6.5–8.0. Deep taproot absorbs subsoil moisture during drought.
• Land Prep: Plough land 2 times followed by harrowing to secure level seedbed.

Fertilizer Recommendations for Sunflower (NPK per Hectare):
• Rainfed Sunflower (Kharif): 60 kg Nitrogen (N) : 60 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 30 kg Potassium (K2O) + 25 kg Elemental Sulfur per hectare.
  - Basal Application: 50% Nitrogen + 100% Phosphorus + 100% Potassium + 100% Sulfur at sowing.
  - Top-Dressing (30 Days After Sowing): 50% Nitrogen at buttoning stage.
• Irrigated Sunflower: 90 kg Nitrogen (N) : 90 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 60 kg Potassium (K2O) + 30 kg Elemental Sulfur per hectare.
• Boron Foliar Spray (MOST CRITICAL for seed setting): Spray Solubor (Boron 20%) @ 2 g/litre water on head/flower disc at ray floret stage (50 DAS) to prevent hollow seed formation and boost seed setting by 25%.

Critical Irrigation & Growth Stages for Sunflower:
1. Germination & Seedling Phase: 0–15 Days After Sowing (DAS).
2. Buttoning / Head Initiation Stage: 30–35 DAS (first top-dressing stage).
3. Flowering / Anthesis Stage: 50–55 DAS (MOST CRITICAL stage — hand pollination using cloth disc or placing honeybee hives @ 2 hives/acre boosts seed set by 30%).
4. Seed Filling & Development Stage: 70–85 DAS (maintain adequate soil moisture).
5. Harvest: 90–105 DAS (harvest when back of sunflower head turns golden yellow and bracts turn brown).

Pest & Disease Management for Sunflower:
• Alternaria Leaf Blight (Alternaria helianthi): Spray Mancozeb 75 WP @ 2.5 g/litre or Propiconazole 25 EC @ 1 ml/litre at 45 DAS and repeat 15 days later.
• Sunflower Necrosis Disease (SND): Control thrips vector by spraying Imidacloprid 17.8 SL @ 0.3 ml/litre or Fipronil 5 SC @ 1 ml/litre at 20 DAS.
• Head Rot (Sclerotinia sclerotiorum): Spray Carbendazim 50 WP @ 1 g/litre; avoid overhead sprinkler irrigation during flowering.
