Chickpea (Gram / Chana) Cultivation Guidelines — National Advisory
Source: ICAR-Indian Institute of Pulses Research (IIPR), Kanpur; ICRISAT Patancheru

Suitable Agro-Climatic Zones for Chickpea (Gram / Chana):
• Top Producing States: Madhya Pradesh (No. 1 chickpea producing state), Maharashtra, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Karnataka (Kalaburagi/Vijayapura/Bidar dryland belt), Andhra Pradesh, and Gujarat.
• Soil & Climate: Well-drained medium to heavy black cotton soils (Vertisols) or clay loams with pH 6.0–8.0. Requires cool winter dry climate; sensitive to excess moisture and soil salinity.

Recommended Chickpea (Gram / Chana) Varieties:
• Desi Chickpea Varieties: JG-11 (most popular variety in South India), JG-16, JAKI-9218, Digvijay, Vijay, RVG-202, BMN-60. Yield: 18–25 q/ha.
• Kabuli Chickpea Varieties (Large Seeded): KAK-2, JGK-1, Vihar, MNK-1 (specially suited for Karnataka), PKV-Kabuli-4. Yield: 20–28 q/ha.

Sowing Seasons & Seed Rate for Chickpea (Gram / Chana):
• Rabi Season: Optimum sowing window October 15 to November 15. Seed rate: Desi Gram: 65–75 kg/ha (30 cm x 10 cm spacing). Kabuli Gram: 90–100 kg/ha (37.5 cm x 10 cm spacing).
• Seed Treatment: Trichoderma viride @ 4 g/kg seed + Rhizobium culture @ 200 g/ha + PSB culture @ 200 g/ha before sowing to prevent root rot and wilt.

Fertilizer Recommendations for Chickpea (Gram / Chana) (NPK per Hectare):
• Rabi Rainfed Chickpea: 20 kg Nitrogen (N) : 50 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 20 kg Potassium (K2O) + 20 kg Elemental Sulfur per hectare.
  - Basal Application: Apply 100% N, P, K, and S at sowing. Chickpea is a pulse crop that fixes atmospheric nitrogen through root nodules; excess N suppresses nodulation.

Critical Irrigation & Growth Stages for Chickpea (Gram / Chana):
1. Germination & Seedling Establishment: 0–15 Days After Sowing (DAS).
2. Branching & Nipping Phase: 30–40 DAS (nipping shoot tips at 35 DAS forces lateral branching and pod setting).
3. Pre-Flowering Stage: 45–50 DAS (critical moisture stage — one irrigation if dry).
4. Pod Development & Grain Filling: 70–85 DAS (avoid heavy watering during peak flowering — causes flower drop).
5. Maturity & Harvest: 100–115 DAS (plants turn dry straw yellow).

Pest & Disease Management for Chickpea (Gram / Chana):
• Gram Pod Borer (Helicoverpa armigera): Install Pheromone traps @ 5/acre at 45 DAS. Spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 0.3 ml/litre or Indoxacarb 14.5 SC @ 0.5 ml/litre or Emamectin benzoate 5 SG @ 0.4 g/litre at 50% flowering.
• Fusarium Wilt (Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. ciceris): Seed treatment with Carbendazim 12% + Mancozeb 63% WP (Saaf) @ 2 g/kg seed; use wilt-resistant varieties JG-11 or JAKI-9218.
• Dry Root Rot (Rhizoctonia bataticola): Soil drenching or seed treatment with Trichoderma viride @ 5 g/kg seed.
