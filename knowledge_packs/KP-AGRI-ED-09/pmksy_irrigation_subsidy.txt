PM Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (PMKSY) — Micro Irrigation & Water Subsidy
Source: Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare; National Mission on Micro Irrigation (NMMI)

Scheme Components & Focus:
• PMKSY tagline: "Har Khet Ko Pani" (water for every field) and "Per Drop More Crop" (micro-irrigation focus).
• Core Goal: Expand cultivated area under assured irrigation, improve water use efficiency, and promote drip & sprinkler irrigation technologies.

Subsidies for Drip & Sprinkler Irrigation (Per Drop More Crop):
• Small & Marginal Farmers (< 2.0 Hectares landholding):
  - Government Subsidy: 55% of the total installation cost for drip & sprinkler systems (enhanced up to 90% in state top-up schemes like Karnataka).
• Other / Large Farmers (> 2.0 Hectares):
  - Government Subsidy: 45% of total installation cost.
• Covered Equipment: Inline drip lateral lines, micro-sprinklers, rain-guns, fertigation tanks, sand/disc filters, hydro-cyclone filters, venturi injectors, and PVC main lines.

Water Harvesting & Farm Pond Subsidies:
• Krishi Bhagya / PMKSY Watershed Component:
  - Farm Ponds (Krishi Konda / Shetketale): Up to 80–90% subsidy for digging lining farm ponds for rainwater harvesting.
  - Diesel / Solar Pump Sets: 50% subsidy for solar water pump sets under PM-KUSUM Component-B (off-grid solar pumps up to 7.5 HP).

Eligibility & Registration Procedure:
• Eligibility: All land-owning farmers, tenant farmers with registered lease agreements, and members of Water Users Associations (WUAs).
• Application Channels: Online portal of State Agriculture / Horticulture Department (e.g. Karnataka DPT/Fruit Portal fruits.karnataka.gov.in) or district Horticulture Officer / Raitha Samparka Kendra (RSK).
• Required Documents: Land Record (Pahani / RTC), Aadhaar Card, Bank Passbook, Soil & Water Testing Report, Water Source Certificate (Borewell / Open Well declaration).
