Onion Cultivation Guidelines — National & Regional Advisory
Source: ICAR-Directorate of Onion and Garlic Research (DOGR), Rajgurunagar (Pune); MPKV Rahuri & UAS Dharwad

Suitable Agro-Climatic Zones for Onion:
• Top Producing States: Maharashtra (No. 1 onion producer in India — Nashik, Ahmednagar, Pune, Solapur belt), Madhya Pradesh, Karnataka (Vijayapura, Bagalkot, Gadag, Chitradurga, Davanagere), Gujarat, Rajasthan, and Bihar.
• Soil & Climate: Well-drained friable red loams, light sandy loams, and loamy soils rich in organic matter with pH 6.0–7.5. Avoid heavy sticky clay soils (impedes bulb development).

Recommended Onion Varieties:
• Kharif Season Varieties (Monsoon): Bhima Super, Bhima Dark Red, Agri-Found Dark Red, Baswant-780, Arka Kalyan. Yield: 25–35 tonnes/ha.
• Late Kharif / Rabi Season Varieties (Winter / Storage): Bhima Shakti, Bhima Kiran, N-2-4-1 (excellent storage quality), Pusa Red, Arka Niketan. Yield: 35–45 tonnes/ha.

Sowing, Nursery & Transplanting for Onion:
• Planting Seasons: Kharif (nursery May–June, transplant July–Aug); Late Kharif (nursery Aug–Sept, transplant Oct–Nov); Rabi (nursery Oct–Nov, transplant Dec–Jan).
• Nursery & Seed Rate: Seed rate: 8–10 kg seed/ha. Nursery period: 45–50 days. Transplant 15 cm seedling at 15 cm x 10 cm spacing on raised beds (1.2 m wide raised beds with drip line).

Fertilizer Recommendations for Onion (NPK per Hectare):
• Irrigated Onion Crop: 100 kg Nitrogen (N) : 50 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 50 kg Potassium (K2O) + 45 kg Elemental Sulfur per hectare.
  - Basal Dose: 50% Nitrogen + 100% Phosphorus + 100% Potassium + 100% Sulfur at transplanting. Sulfur is MOST CRITICAL for pungency, bulb size, and storage life in onion.
  - Top-Dressing (30 Days After Transplanting - DAT): 25% Nitrogen.
  - Top-Dressing (45 DAT): Remaining 25% Nitrogen. Stop nitrogen top-dressing after 45 DAT to prevent thick necking and poor bulb storage.

Critical Irrigation & Growth Stages for Onion:
1. Transplanting & Seedling Establishment: 0–15 DAT.
2. Vegetative Leaf Stage: 15–45 DAT.
3. Bulb Initiation & Development Stage: 45–75 DAT (MOST CRITICAL water stage — water stress causes small split bulbs).
4. Bulb Maturation & Neck Fall: 90–110 DAT (stop irrigation 15–20 days prior to harvest when 50% neck fall occurs). Field curing for 3 days followed by shade curing for 10 days before storage.

Pest & Disease Management for Onion:
• Onion Thrips (Thrips tabaci — Vector of Iris Yellow Spot Virus): Spray Fipronil 5 SC @ 1 ml/litre or Spinetoram 11.7 SC @ 0.8 ml/litre or Profenofos 50 EC @ 1.5 ml/litre + Sticker @ 1 ml/litre (waxy leaves require sticker).
• Purple Blotch (Alternaria porri) & Stemphylium Blight: Spray Mancozeb 75 WP @ 2.5 g/litre or Tebuconazole + Trifloxystrobin (Nativo) @ 0.6 g/litre.
