Mustard & Rapeseed Cultivation Guidelines — National Advisory
Source: ICAR-Directorate of Rapeseed-Mustard Research (DRMR), Bharatpur; SKRAU Bikaner & PAU Ludhiana

Suitable Agro-Climatic Zones for Mustard & Rapeseed:
• Top Producing States: Rajasthan (No. 1 mustard producing state in India, accounting for >45% production), Madhya Pradesh, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, Punjab, and Gujarat.
• Soil & Climate: Well-drained sandy loam to medium clay loam soils with pH 6.5–8.0. Requires cool dry winter climate (Rabi crop); sensitive to frost during flowering.

Recommended Mustard & Rapeseed Varieties:
• Indian Mustard (Rai) Varieties: Pusa Mustard-25 (NPJ-112), Pusa Mustard-28, DRMRIJ-31 (Giriraj), RH-749, NRCDR-02, PM-30 (low erucic acid). Yield: 20–28 q/ha seed with 38–42% oil content.
• Yellow Sarson / Toria Varieties: NRCYS-05, YSH-04, PT-303, T-9. Yield: 15–22 q/ha.

Sowing Seasons & Seed Rate for Mustard & Rapeseed:
• Rabi Season: Optimum sowing window October 1 to October 25 (sowing when temperature drops to 25–28°C). Seed rate: 4–5 kg seed/ha (45 cm x 15 cm spacing; 2–3 cm depth).
• Seed Treatment: Trichoderma viride @ 6 g/kg seed or Metalaxyl 35 SD @ 6 g/kg seed to prevent seedling blight and white rust.

Fertilizer Recommendations for Mustard & Rapeseed (NPK per Hectare):
• Irrigated Mustard: 80 kg Nitrogen (N) : 40 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 40 kg Potassium (K2O) + 40 kg Elemental Sulfur per hectare.
  - Basal Application: Apply 50% Nitrogen + full Phosphorus + full Potassium + full Sulfur at sowing. Sulfur is MOST CRITICAL for oil synthesis in mustard.
  - Top-Dressing (30–35 Days After Sowing): Apply remaining 50% Nitrogen at first irrigation (rosette stage).
• Rainfed Mustard: 40 kg Nitrogen (N) : 20 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 20 kg Potassium (K2O) + 20 kg Elemental Sulfur per hectare.

Critical Irrigation & Growth Stages for Mustard & Rapeseed:
1. Germination & Rosette Stage: 0–25 Days After Sowing (DAS).
2. Stem Elongation / Branching Stage: 30–35 DAS (MOST CRITICAL for first irrigation).
3. Flowering & Siliqua (Pod) Formation Stage: 50–65 DAS (second irrigation stage).
4. Pod Filling & Maturity: 80–100 DAS (harvest when 75% pods turn golden yellow).

Pest & Disease Management for Mustard & Rapeseed:
• Mustard Aphid (Lipaphis erysimi — Major Pest): Spray Dimethoate 30 EC @ 1.7 ml/litre water or Imidacloprid 17.8 SL @ 0.3 ml/litre or Thiamethoxam 25 WG @ 0.2 g/litre when aphid population exceeds 20/plant at flowering.
• White Rust (Albugo candida) & Alternaria Blight: Spray Mancozeb 75 WP @ 2.5 g/litre or Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP (Ridomil Gold) @ 2 g/litre at 45 DAS.
• Painted Bug & Sawfly: Apply Chlorpyriphos 20 EC @ 2 ml/litre at early seedling stage.
