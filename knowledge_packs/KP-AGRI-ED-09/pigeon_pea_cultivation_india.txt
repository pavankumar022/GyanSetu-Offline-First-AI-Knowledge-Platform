Tur (Pigeon Pea / Arhar) Cultivation Guidelines — National & Karnataka Advisory
Source: ICAR-Indian Institute of Pulses Research (IIPR), Kanpur; UAS Raichur & Kalaburagi Pulse Research Station

Suitable Agro-Climatic Zones for Tur (Pigeon Pea / Arhar):
• Top Producing States: Maharashtra (No. 1 tur producing state), Karnataka (Kalaburagi — known as the "Pulse Bowl of Karnataka"), Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Gujarat, Telangana, and Andhra Pradesh.
• Karnataka Tur Belts: Kalaburagi (GI tagged "Kalaburagi Red Gram"), Bidar, Yadgir, Raichur, Vijayapura, and Belagavi.

Recommended Tur (Pigeon Pea / Arhar) Varieties:
• Wilt Resistant & High Yielding Varieties: GRG-811 (Bhima — wilt & sterility mosaic resistant variety developed by UAS Raichur), BSMR-736, Maruti (ICP-8863), TS-3R, ICPH-2671 (hybrid tur), WBG-26, BDN-711. Yield: 18–25 q/ha grain.

Sowing Seasons & Seed Rate for Tur (Pigeon Pea / Arhar):
• Kharif Season: Optimum window June 15 to July 15 (with arrival of monsoon). Seed rate: 12–15 kg seed/ha for pure cropping (90 cm x 30 cm spacing); 5 kg/ha for dibbling at wide spacing (120 cm x 30 cm).
• Seed Treatment: Bio-fertilizer treatment with Rhizobium culture @ 200 g + Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria (PSB) @ 200 g per 10 kg seed before sowing.

Soil & Land Preparation for Tur (Pigeon Pea / Arhar):
• Soil Type: Deep well-drained black cotton soils (Vertisols) or medium loamy soils with pH 6.5–7.8. Deep taproot system withstands dryland moisture stress.
• Land Prep: Deep ploughing to allow deep taproot development. Construct ridges and furrows to drain excess rainwater.

Fertilizer Recommendations for Tur (Pigeon Pea / Arhar) (NPK per Hectare):
• Rainfed Tur (Kharif Pulse): 25 kg Nitrogen (N) : 50 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 25 kg Potassium (K2O) + 20 kg Elemental Sulfur per hectare.
  - Basal Dose: 100% N, P, K, and S applied at time of sowing.
  - Nipping Shoot Tops: Nipping / pruning the top growing tip at 45–50 Days After Sowing (DAS) forces lateral branching and increases pod count by 20–25%.

Critical Irrigation & Growth Stages for Tur (Pigeon Pea / Arhar):
1. Germination & Seedling Phase: 0–20 DAS.
2. Branching & Vegetative Growth: 30–50 DAS (nipping top tip at 45 DAS).
3. Flower Bud Initiation & Peak Flowering: 75–90 DAS (MOST CRITICAL water requirement stage).
4. Pod Formation & Grain Development: 105–130 DAS (protective irrigation during drought boosts grain weight).
5. Harvest: 150–180 DAS (when 80% pods turn brown and dry).

Pest & Disease Management for Tur (Pigeon Pea / Arhar):
• Pod Borer (Helicoverpa armigera & Maruca vitrata): Install Pheromone traps @ 5/acre at flowering stage. Spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 0.3 ml/litre or Indoxacarb 14.5 SC @ 0.5 ml/litre or Spinetoram 11.7 SC @ 0.8 ml/litre at 50% flowering stage.
• Fusarium Wilt (Fusarium udum): Soil drenching or seed treatment with Trichoderma harzianum @ 10 g/kg seed; use wilt-resistant varieties GRG-811 or Maruti.
• Sterility Mosaic Disease (SMD): Control vector mite by spraying Propargite 57 EC @ 2 ml/litre.
