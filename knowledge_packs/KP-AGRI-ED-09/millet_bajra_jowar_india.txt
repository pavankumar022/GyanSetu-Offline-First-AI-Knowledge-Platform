Millet Cultivation (Bajra Pearl Millet & Jowar Sorghum) — National Advisory
Source: ICAR-Indian Institute of Millets Research (IIMR), Hyderabad; ICRISAT Patancheru

Suitable Agro-Climatic Zones for Millets (Bajra & Jowar):
• Pearl Millet (Bajra): Rajasthan (No. 1 bajra producing state), Maharashtra, Gujarat, Haryana, Uttar Pradesh, and Northern Dry Zone of Karnataka (Vijayapura, Bagalkot). Extremely drought-hardy, thrives in low rainfall (<400 mm) arid zones.
• Sorghum (Jowar): Maharashtra (No. 1 jowar producing state), Karnataka (Northern dry zone — Solapur/Kalaburagi belt), Rajasthan, MP, Andhra Pradesh, and Telangana.

Recommended Millet Varieties:
• Pearl Millet (Bajra) Hybrids: HHB-67 Improved, RHB-177, MPMH-17, Pioneer 86M84, Proagro 9444. Yield: 25–35 q/ha grain.
• Sorghum (Jowar) Varieties:
  - Rabi Jowar (Dryland): CSV-22R, M35-1 (Maldandi — premium grain & fodder quality), Phule Anuradha, CSV-29R. Yield: 20–28 q/ha.
  - Kharif Jowar Hybrids: CSH-16, CSH-25, CSV-15. Yield: 35–45 q/ha.

Sowing Seasons & Seed Rate for Millets (Bajra & Jowar):
• Kharif Bajra / Jowar: Sowing window June 15 to July 15. Bajra seed rate: 4 kg/ha (45 cm x 15 cm spacing). Jowar seed rate: 8–10 kg/ha (45 cm x 15 cm spacing).
• Rabi Jowar (Dryland Vertisols): September 15 to October 15 (sowing in conserved soil moisture after monsoons).

Fertilizer Recommendations for Millets (Bajra & Jowar) (NPK per Hectare):
• Bajra (Pearl Millet): 80 kg Nitrogen (N) : 40 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 40 kg Potassium (K2O). Apply 50% N + full P & K at sowing; top dress 50% N at 30 DAS after weeding.
• Jowar (Sorghum):
  - Rainfed Rabi Jowar: 50 kg Nitrogen (N) : 25 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 25 kg Potassium (K2O) as full basal dose at sowing.
  - Irrigated Kharif Jowar: 100 kg Nitrogen (N) : 50 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 40 kg Potassium (K2O).

Critical Irrigation & Growth Stages for Millets (Bajra & Jowar):
1. Seedling Establishment: 0–15 Days After Sowing (DAS).
2. Tillering / Knee-High Stage: 25–30 DAS.
3. Boot Stage / Flowering: 45–55 DAS (MOST CRITICAL moisture stage — one protective irrigation doubles yield during dry spells).
4. Grain Dough & Maturity: 75–90 DAS (harvest when grain moisture drops below 15%).

Pest & Disease Management for Millets (Bajra & Jowar):
• Jowar Shoot Fly (Atherigona soccata): Early sowing within 7 days of monsoon onset; seed treatment with Imidacloprid 70 WS @ 10 g/kg seed before sowing.
• Bajra Downy Mildew (Green Ear): Seed treatment with Metalaxyl 35 SD @ 6 g/kg seed; rogue out infected plants at 30 DAS.
• Grain Mold (Fusarium / Curvularia on Jowar): Spray Propiconazole 25 EC @ 1 ml/litre at flowering if late rains occur.
