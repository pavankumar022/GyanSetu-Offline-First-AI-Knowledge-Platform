Soybean Cultivation Guidelines — National & Regional Advisory
Source: ICAR-Indian Institute of Soybean Research (IISR), Indore; MPKV Rahuri & UAS Dharwad

Suitable Agro-Climatic Zones for Soybean:
• Top Producing States: Madhya Pradesh (known as the "Soy State" — Malwa plateau region), Maharashtra (Latur, Nanded, Amravati), Rajasthan (Kota region), Karnataka (Belagavi, Bidar, Dharwad), and Telangana.
• Soil Requirements: Deep, well-drained medium to heavy black cotton soils (Vertisols) or loamy soils with high organic matter and pH 6.5–7.5. Sensitive to waterlogging.

Recommended Soybean Varieties:
• High Yielding Varieties: JS-335 (most popular variety in Central India), JS-95-60 (early maturing 80–85 days), JS-20-34, JS-20-29, DSb-21, DSb-23 (rust resistant for South India), MACS-1407, NRC-37 (Ahilya-4). Yield: 20–30 q/ha grain.

Sowing Seasons & Seed Rate for Soybean:
• Kharif Season: Optimum sowing window June 15 to July 10 (with arrival of monsoon rains). Seed rate: 70–75 kg seed/ha (45 cm x 5 cm spacing; 3–4 cm depth).
• Seed Treatment: Bio-fertilizer inoculation with Bradyrhizobium japonicum @ 5 g/kg seed + Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria (PSB) @ 5 g/kg seed + Carboxin 37.5% + Thiram 37.5% @ 3 g/kg seed before sowing.

Fertilizer Recommendations for Soybean (NPK per Hectare):
• Kharif Rainfed Soybean: 20 kg Nitrogen (N) : 60 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 40 kg Potassium (K2O) + 20 kg Elemental Sulfur per hectare.
  - Basal Application: Apply 100% N, P, K, and S at sowing. Soybean fixes atmospheric nitrogen via root nodules; excess N fertilizer reduces nodulation efficiency.

Critical Irrigation & Growth Stages for Soybean:
1. Germination & Seedling Phase: 0–15 Days After Sowing (DAS).
2. Branching & Vegetative Growth: 25–35 DAS.
3. Flowering Stage: 40–50 DAS (MOST CRITICAL for moisture — water stress causes flower dropping).
4. Pod Development & Grain Filling: 60–75 DAS (maintain adequate moisture).
5. Physiological Maturity: 90–100 DAS (leaves turn yellow and drop off; pods turn brown).

Pest & Disease Management for Soybean:
• Girdle Beetle (Oberia brevis) & Stem Fly: Spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 0.3 ml/litre water or Thiamethoxam 12.6% + Lambda-cyhalothrin 9.5% ZC @ 0.25 ml/litre at 30 DAS.
• Soybean Rust (Phakopsora pachyrhizi): Spray Hexaconazole 5 EC @ 1 ml/litre or Propiconazole 25 EC @ 1 ml/litre at first appearance of rust spots; use rust-resistant varieties DSb-21 or DSb-23.
• Tobacco Caterpillar (Spodoptera litura) & Semilooper: Spray Flubendiamide 39.35 SC @ 0.3 ml/litre.
