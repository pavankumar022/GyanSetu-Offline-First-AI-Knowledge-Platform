Sesame (Til) & Castor Cultivation Guidelines — National & Regional Advisory
Source: ICAR-Directorate of Oilseeds Research (DOR), Hyderabad; JAU Junagadh & UAS Raichur

Suitable Agro-Climatic Zones & Soil Requirements:
• Zones & Regions: Gujarat (Saurashtra castor & sesame hub), Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, West Bengal, Karnataka (Raichur, Koppal, Bellary), Andhra Pradesh, and Tamil Nadu.
• Soil & pH: Well-drained light sandy loams to medium red loamy soils with pH 6.5–7.8. Highly drought-tolerant oilseed crops; sensitive to soil salinity.

Recommended Varieties by Season:
• Sesame (Til) Varieties:
  - Kharif / Summer: RT-351, TKG-22, GT-3, DS-5 (for Karnataka). Seed color: White/Brown. Yield: 8–12 q/ha.
• Castor Varieties & Hybrids:
  - Kharif Rainfed / Irrigated: GCH-7, GCH-9, DCH-519, DCH-177, 48-1 (Jwala). Yield: 20–30 q/ha.

Sowing Seasons & Planting Window:
• Kharif Season: June 15 to July 15 (with onset of monsoons).
• Summer Sesame (Zaid): January 15 to February 15 (irrigated crop).
• Rabi / Semi-Rabi Castor: August 15 to September 15.

Row Spacing & Seed Rate:
• Sesame (Til) Seed Rate: 4–5 kg seed/ha (30 cm x 10 cm spacing; 1.5–2.0 cm depth mixed with fine sand).
• Castor Seed Rate: Hybrids: 5 kg/ha (90 cm x 60 cm spacing for rainfed; 120 cm x 90 cm for irrigated).

Irrigation Requirement & Water Management:
• Water Type: Rainfed for Kharif; Light Drip / Furrow Irrigated for Summer Sesame & Irrigated Castor.
• Critical Growth Stages needing Irrigation:
  - Sesame: Flowering (30 DAS) and Capsule Formation (45–50 DAS). Stop irrigation when leaves start yellowing.
  - Castor: Main Spike Flowering (45 DAS) and Secondary Spike Bulking (75–90 DAS). Extremely drought-hardy.

Fertilizer & NPK Recommendations (per Hectare):
• Sesame (Til): 40 kg Nitrogen (N) : 20 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 20 kg Potassium (K2O) + 20 kg Elemental Sulfur per hectare.
• Castor (Irrigated Hybrid): 80 kg Nitrogen (N) : 40 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 30 kg Potassium (K2O) + 30 kg Sulfur per hectare. Split N in 3 doses (basal, 30 DAS, and 60 DAS).

Growth Stages & Duration:
1. Emergence & Seedling Phase: 0–10 Days After Sowing (DAS).
2. Vegetative Branching: 15–35 DAS.
3. Flowering & Spike/Capsule Formation: 40–60 DAS.
4. Maturity & Harvest: 80–90 DAS for Sesame; 150–180 DAS for Castor (multiple spike pickings).

Common Pest Control (Insect Pests):
• Sesame Leaf & Pod Borer (Antigastra catalaunalis): Spray Quinalphos 25 EC @ 2 ml/litre or Chlorpyriphos 20 EC @ 2 ml/litre at 30 DAS.
• Castor Semilooper & Capsule Borer (Conogethes punctiferalis): Handpick large caterpillars; spray Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt) @ 2 g/litre or Profenofos 50 EC @ 2 ml/litre.

Common Disease Management (Fungal & Phytoplasma):
• Sesame Phyllody (Phytoplasma — Leafhopper Vector): Rogue out infected bushy green floral structures; spray Dimethoate 30 EC @ 1.7 ml/litre water to control vector leafhoppers.
• Castor Fusarium Wilt & Botrytis Gray Rot: Seed treatment with Trichoderma viride @ 10 g/kg seed; spray Carbendazim 50 WP @ 1 g/litre during rainy spells.

Expected Yield:
• Sesame: 8–12 q/ha. Castor: 20–30 q/ha.

Top Producing States:
• Gujarat (No. 1 castor & sesame producer in India), Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, West Bengal, Karnataka.
