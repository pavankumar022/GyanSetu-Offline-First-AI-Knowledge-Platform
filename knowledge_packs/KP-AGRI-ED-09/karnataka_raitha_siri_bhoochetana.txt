Karnataka State Farmer Schemes — Raitha Siri, Bhoochetana & e-NAM Agri Marketing
Source: Department of Agriculture, Government of Karnataka; e-NAM Portal (enam.gov.in)

1. Raitha Siri Scheme (Millet Farmers Incentive):
• Objective: Promote climate-resilient millet cultivation (Ragi, Bajra, Jowar, Foxtail millet, Navane, Same, Haraka) across rainfed dryland districts of Karnataka.
• Financial Incentive: Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) of ₹10,000 per hectare directly into the farmer's Aadhaar-linked bank account upon verification of millet sowing in FRUITS portal.
• Eligibility: All millet growing farmers in Karnataka registered with FRUITS ID.

2. Bhoochetana Programme (Soil Health & Micronutrient Subsidy):
• Objective: Rejuvenate dryland soil fertility across all 30 districts of Karnataka through balanced nutrient application.
• Subsidies & Benefits:
  - 50% subsidy on Micronutrients (Zinc Sulfate, Borax, Gypsum).
  - 50% subsidy on Bio-fertilizers (Rhizobium, Azospirillum, PSB, Trichoderma viride).
  - Free soil testing and Soil Health Card distribution through Raitha Samparka Kendras (RSK).

3. e-NAM (National Agriculture Market) & ReMS Rashtriya e-Market Services:
• Overview: Unified online trading portal connecting APMC mandis across Karnataka (Unified Market Platform - UMP) and national mandis.
• Benefits for Farmers:
  - Electronic transparent bidding for agricultural produce (prevents trader cartelization).
  - Direct Online Payment into farmer's bank account within 24 hours of trade settlement.
  - Free Assay / Quality Testing at APMC assaying labs before auction.
• How to Register: Visit local APMC mandi with Aadhaar Card, Bank Passbook, and Pahani / RTC or register online on enam.gov.in.
