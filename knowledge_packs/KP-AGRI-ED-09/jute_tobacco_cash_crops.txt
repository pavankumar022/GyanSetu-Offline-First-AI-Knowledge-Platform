Jute & Tobacco Cash Crops Cultivation Guidelines — National Advisory
Source: ICAR-Central Research Institute for Jute and Allied Fibres (CRIJAF), Barrackpore; ICAR-CTRI Rajahmundry

Suitable Agro-Climatic Zones & Soil Requirements:
• Jute: West Bengal (No. 1 jute producer — Hooghly, Nadia, Murshidabad), Assam, Bihar, Odisha, and Tripura. Rich alluvial loamy soils with pH 6.0–7.5; requires hot humid climate (>70% humidity) and high rainfall.
• Tobacco (FCV & Bidi Tobacco): Andhra Pradesh (Guntur/Prakasam FCV belt), Karnataka (Mysuru/Hunsur light soil FCV belt), Gujarat (Anand/Kheda Bidi belt), and Bihar. Light sandy loams to red sandy loams with pH 5.5–6.5.

Recommended Varieties by Season:
• Jute Varieties (Tossa & White Jute): JRO-204 (Hooghly-1), JRO-5408, JRO-8432, JRC-698 (Samresh). Yield: 30–40 q/ha dry fibre.
• Tobacco Varieties (FCV & Bidi): Kanchan, Siri, VT-1158, N-98 (FCV Tobacco); Anand-119, ABT-10 (Bidi Tobacco). Yield: 20–25 q/ha cured leaf.

Sowing Seasons & Planting Window:
• Jute Sowing Window: March 15 to April 30 (pre-monsoon showers).
• Tobacco Nursery & Transplanting: Nursery sowing July–August; Transplanting September 15 to October 15 (Rabi crop).

Row Spacing & Seed Rate:
• Jute Seed Rate: 5–6 kg seed/ha for Tossa Jute (20 cm x 5 cm spacing). Seed treatment with Carbendazim @ 2 g/kg seed.
• Tobacco Seed Rate & Spacing: Seed rate: 30–40 g nursery seed for 1 hectare. Transplant 60-day old seedlings at 100 cm x 60 cm spacing (FCV) or 75 cm x 75 cm (Bidi).

Irrigation Requirement & Water Management:
• Water Type: Rainfed/Submerged for Jute; Irrigated Light Furrow for Tobacco.
• Critical Growth Stages needing Irrigation:
  - Jute: Requires 2–3 protective irrigations during early pre-monsoon establishment; rainfed afterwards. Retting requires clean slow-flowing water bodies.
  - Tobacco: 3–4 light irrigations during grand vegetative growth and topping stage (45–60 DAT). Avoid over-watering (causes wet-foot wilt).

Fertilizer & NPK Recommendations (per Hectare):
• Jute Dose: 80 kg Nitrogen (N) : 40 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 40 kg Potassium (K2O). Split N in 2 doses (basal and 3 weeks after weeding).
• Tobacco (FCV Light Soil): 40 kg Nitrogen (N) : 60 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 120 kg Potassium (K2O) (High Potash Potassium Sulfate form mandatory for burn quality; avoid Muriate of Potash / Chloride).

Growth Stages & Duration:
1. Germination & Nursery Stage: 0–20 Days for Jute; 0–60 Days Nursery for Tobacco.
2. Vegetative Fiber / Leaf Elongation: 30–90 Days.
3. Topping & Desuckering (Tobacco): At 55–60 DAT (remove floral heads to divert energy to leaves).
4. Harvesting & Processing: Jute harvested at 120 days (small pod stage) followed by 15-day water retting and ribboning. Tobacco harvested by priming 2–4 leaves every 8 days (120–140 DAT) followed by flue-curing / sun-curing.

Common Pest Control (Insect Pests):
• Jute Hairy Caterpillar & Semilooper: Spray Profenofos 50 EC @ 2 ml/litre or Lambda-cyhalothrin 5 EC @ 1 ml/litre.
• Tobacco Caterpillar (Spodoptera litura) & Aphids: Spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 0.3 ml/litre or Imidacloprid 17.8 SL @ 0.3 ml/litre.

Common Disease Management (Fungal & Viral):
• Jute Stem Rot & Root Rot (Macrophomina phaseolina): Seed treatment with Carbendazim @ 2 g/kg; soil drenching with Copper Oxychloride @ 3 g/litre.
• Tobacco Black Shank & Damping Off: Drench nursery beds with Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2 g/litre.

Expected Yield:
• Jute Fibre: 30–40 q/ha. Tobacco Cured Leaf: 20–25 q/ha.

Top Producing States:
• Jute: West Bengal, Assam, Bihar. Tobacco: Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat, Karnataka.
