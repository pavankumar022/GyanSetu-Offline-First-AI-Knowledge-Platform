Farmer Distress Helplines, Free Legal Aid & Debt Relief Rights Guide
Source: National Legal Services Authority (NALSA); Ministry of Agriculture & Tele-MANAS Helpline

1. Free Legal Aid Rights (Legal Services Authorities Act, 1987):
• Free Legal Representation: Small & Marginal Farmers, SC/ST citizens, women, and low-income rural households are legally entitled to FREE legal representation, court fee waiver, and advocate drafting services in civil, criminal, and land dispute cases.
• How to Access Free Legal Aid: Contact Member Secretary, District Legal Services Authority (DLSA) at District Court complex, or Taluk Legal Services Committee (TLSC) at Taluk court.
• NALSA Toll-Free Legal Aid Helpline: 15100 (24x7 free legal helpline).

2. Kisan Call Centre (KCC Helpline):
• Toll-Free Number: 1800-180-1551 (6:00 AM to 10:00 PM, 365 days a year).
• Services Provided: Free advice in 22 local Indian languages by agricultural graduate experts on crop advisory, pest outbreaks, weather forecasts, market prices (Mandi rates), and government subsidies.

3. Farmer Mental Health & Distress Suicide Prevention Helpline:
• Tele-MANAS (National Tele Mental Health Programme): Toll-free 14416 or 1800-891-4416 (24x7 free confidential mental health counseling in 20 languages).
• Kiran Mental Health Helpline: 1800-599-0019.

4. Agricultural Debt Relief & One-Time Settlement (OTS) Rights:
• Moneylender Exorbitant Interest Protection: Private moneylenders charging interest above state-capped limits (typically >18% per annum) are illegal under State Money Lenders Acts. Registered money lenders cannot seize agricultural land without court order.
• RBI Debt Restructuring Guidelines during Natural Calamity: When crop loss exceeds 33% due to flood/drought, banks are mandated to convert short-term crop loans into long-term loans (3–5 years repayment period) with 1-year moratorium on principal.
