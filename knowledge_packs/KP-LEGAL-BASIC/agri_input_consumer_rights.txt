Agricultural Inputs Consumer Rights & Seed/Fertilizer Quality Complaint SOP
Source: Seeds Act 1966; Fertilizer Control Order (FCO) 1985; Consumer Protection Act 2019

1. Rights Against Substandard Seeds (Seeds Act, 1966):
• Quality Guarantee: Every seed packet sold must display Variety Name, Lot Number, Germination Percentage (min 70-80%), Physical Purity (min 98%), Moisture Content, and Expiry Date.
• Compensation Right for Seed Failure: If certified seeds fail to germinate or yield due to genetic defect, farmers can file a joint complaint with the District Seed Inspector / Agriculture Officer (AO).
• Inspection & Sample Collection: Seed Inspector must draw 3 sealed samples in farmer's presence and send to State Seed Testing Laboratory (STL) within 24 hours. STL report must be issued within 30 days.

2. Rights Under Fertilizer Control Order (FCO, 1985):
• Standardized Pricing & Receipt: Dealers must sell fertilizers (Urea, DAP, NPK, MOP) at or below Maximum Retail Price (MRP) printed on bag and issue a printed Cash Memo receipt mentioning Lot/Batch number.
• Overcharging & Black Marketing Complaint: Overcharging or compulsory bundling of unwanted products (e.g. forcing farmer to buy nano-urea with DAP) is an offense under Essential Commodities Act. File complaint with District Fertilizer Inspector or Agriculture Officer.

3. Consumer Protection Rights for Farmers (Consumer Protection Act, 2019):
• Farmers as Consumers: Farmers buying seeds, pesticides, fertilizers, drip equipment, or tractors for self-employment/cultivation are legally classified as "Consumers."
• Filing Complaint in Consumer Forum (District Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission):
  - Claims up to ₹50 Lakh filed in District Commission.
  - Required Evidence: Purchase Cash Memo/Bill, Seed Bag tag/packet, Soil/Crop Inspection Report by Agriculture Officer, and Revenue RTC/Pahani showing crop area.
  - National Consumer Helpline Toll-Free: 1915 or 1800-11-4000.
