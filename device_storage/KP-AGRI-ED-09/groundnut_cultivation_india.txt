Groundnut (Peanut) Cultivation Guidelines — National & Karnataka Advisory
Source: ICAR-Directorate of Groundnut Research (DGR), Junagadh; UAS Dharwad & Raichur

Suitable Agro-Climatic Zones for Groundnut (Peanut):
• Top Producing States: Gujarat (Saurashtra region — No. 1 groundnut producer), Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh (Rayalaseema / Anantapur), Karnataka, and Madhya Pradesh.
• Karnataka Groundnut Belts: Chitradurga, Tumakuru, Kolar, Bellary, Raichur, Dharwad, Belagavi, and Vijayapura.

Recommended Groundnut (Peanut) Varieties:
• Rainfed Kharif Varieties: K6 (Kadiri-6), GPBD-4 (foliar disease resistant — high yielding variety from UAS Dharwad), TMV-2, JL-24, DH-86, G2-52. Yield: 18–25 q/ha pods.
• Irrigated Rabi / Summer Varieties: TAG-24, TG-37A, Kadiri-9, ICGV-91114, Dharani (TCGS-888). Yield: 30–42 q/ha pods.

Sowing Seasons & Seed Rate for Groundnut (Peanut):
• Kharif Season: June 15 to July 15 (with onset of monsoon). Seed rate: 100–125 kg kernel/ha (30 cm x 10 cm spacing).
• Summer Groundnut (Irrigated): January 15 to February 15.

Soil & Land Preparation for Groundnut (Peanut):
• Soil Type: Well-drained sandy loam, light red soils, and loamy soils with pH 6.0–7.5. Loose friable surface soil is essential for easy peg penetration and pod development. Avoid sticky clay soils.
• Land Prep: 2 deep ploughings followed by harrowing to break clods into fine tilth.

Fertilizer Recommendations for Groundnut (Peanut) (NPK per Hectare):
• Rainfed Groundnut (Kharif): 25 kg Nitrogen (N) : 50 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 25 kg Potassium (K2O).
  - Basal Application: Apply 100% N, P, and K at time of sowing.
  - Gypsum Application (MOST CRITICAL): Apply Gypsum @ 500 kg/ha at pegging stage (35–40 Days After Sowing - DAS) near plant rows and incorporate lightly. Gypsum provides essential Calcium for pod filling and Sulfur for oil synthesis (prevents pops/empty pods).
• Irrigated Summer Groundnut: 40 kg Nitrogen (N) : 80 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 40 kg Potassium (K2O) + Gypsum @ 500 kg/ha.

Critical Irrigation & Growth Stages for Groundnut (Peanut):
1. Germination & Vegetative Growth: 0–25 DAS.
2. Flowering Stage: 25–30 DAS.
3. Peg Penetration Stage: 35–45 DAS (DO NOT perform inter-cultivation or hoeing after pegging starts — breaks tender pegs entering soil).
4. Pod Development & Filling Stage: 50–75 DAS (MOST CRITICAL moisture stage — water stress causes pops/empty shells).
5. Pod Maturation & Harvest: 95–110 DAS (harvest when inside shell turns dark brown).

Pest & Disease Management for Groundnut (Peanut):
• Tikka Leaf Spot (Cercospora arachidicola & Cercosporidium personatum): Spray Hexaconazole 5 EC @ 1 ml/litre or Chlorothalonil 75 WP @ 2 g/litre or Tebconazole @ 1 ml/litre at 40 DAS.
• Collar Rot / Root Rot (Aspergillus niger): Seed treatment with Trichoderma viride @ 10 g/kg seed or Mancozeb 75 WP @ 3 g/kg seed before sowing.
• Red Hairy Caterpillar & Spodoptera: Spray Chlorpyriphos 20 EC @ 2 ml/litre or Novaluron 10 EC @ 1 ml/litre.
