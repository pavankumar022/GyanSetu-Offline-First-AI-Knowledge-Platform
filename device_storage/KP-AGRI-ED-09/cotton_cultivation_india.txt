Cotton Cultivation Guidelines — National & Karnataka Advisory
Source: ICAR-Central Institute for Cotton Research (CICR), Nagpur; UAS Dharwad (Department of Agronomy & Entomology)

Suitable Agro-Climatic Zones for Cotton:
• Top Producing States: Gujarat (No. 1 cotton producing state), Maharashtra, Telangana, Rajasthan, Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Haryana, Punjab, and Madhya Pradesh.
• Karnataka Cotton Belts: Dharwad (Cotton hub), Haveri, Belagavi, Raichur, Kalaburagi, Bellary, Vijayapura, and Mysore.

Recommended Cotton Hybrids & Varieties:
• Bt Cotton Hybrids (Bollgard II / BG-II): Rasi 659, Bunny Bt, Kanaka Bt, DHH-11, Pioneer 30V92, Bio 9681, ATM-Bt. Yield: 25–35 q/ha seed cotton.
• Rainfed / Desi Cotton Varieties: Jayadhar (herbaceum rainfed for black cotton soils), Suvin (extra-long staple), DLSa-17. Yield: 15–22 q/ha.

Sowing Seasons & Spacing for Cotton:
• Kharif Season (Rainfed / Irrigated): May 15 to June 30 (with arrival of pre-monsoon or monsoon showers).
• Row Spacing & Seed Rate: Bt Cotton Hybrids: 90 cm x 60 cm (2.25 kg seed/ha). Desi Cotton: 60 cm x 30 cm (10 kg seed/ha).

Soil & Land Preparation for Cotton:
• Soil Type: Deep black cotton soils (Vertisols) with high water retention capacity or deep fertile alluvial loams with pH 6.5–8.0.
• Land Prep: Deep ploughing every 3 years to break hardpan followed by 2 harrowings. Form ridges and furrows for proper field drainage.

Fertilizer Recommendations for Cotton (NPK per Hectare):
• Irrigated Bt Cotton Hybrids: 150 kg Nitrogen (N) : 75 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 75 kg Potassium (K2O).
  - Basal Application: 20% Nitrogen + 100% Phosphorus + 33% Potassium at sowing.
  - Square Formation Stage (40–45 Days After Sowing - DAS): 40% Nitrogen + 33% Potassium.
  - Peak Flowering & Boll Formation (70–75 DAS): 40% Nitrogen + 34% Potassium.
  - Magnesium & Boron Foliar Spray: Spray Magnesium Sulfate @ 1% + Borax @ 0.2% at peak flowering to prevent red leaf disease (leaf reddening).
• Rainfed Cotton: 90 kg Nitrogen (N) : 45 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 45 kg Potassium (K2O) per hectare.

Critical Irrigation & Growth Stages for Cotton:
1. Seedling & Vegetative Stage: 0–35 DAS.
2. Square Formation (Flower Bud Emergence): 40–55 DAS.
3. Flowering & Early Boll Development: 60–90 DAS (MOST CRITICAL for moisture — water shortage causes severe flower & boll dropping).
4. Boll Development & Maturation: 90–120 DAS.
5. Boll Bursting & Cotton Picking: 120–160 DAS (picking when bolls open fully; avoid picking morning dew cotton).

Pest & Disease Management for Cotton:
• Pink Bollworm (Pectinophora gossypiella): Install Pheromone traps @ 5/acre at 45 DAS. Spray Spinetoram 11.7 SC @ 1 ml/litre or Profenofos 50 EC @ 2 ml/litre at 10% pink bollworm infestation in green bolls.
• Sucking Pests (Jassids, Thrips, Whitefly): Seed treatment with Imidacloprid 70 WS @ 7 g/kg seed; spray Flonicamid 50 WG @ 0.3 g/litre or Diafenthiuron 50 WP @ 1 g/litre.
• Cotton Leaf Curl Virus (CLCuV) & Wilt: Control whitefly vector; drench soil with Carbendazim @ 1 g/litre.
