Ragi (Finger Millet) Cultivation Guidelines — Karnataka & All-India Advisory
Source: ICAR-Indian Institute of Millets Research (IIMR), Hyderabad; UAS Bengaluru (Project on Small Millets)

Suitable Agro-Climatic Zones for Ragi (Finger Millet):
• Karnataka Zones: Zone 4 (Central Dry Zone), Zone 5 (Eastern Dry Zone), Zone 6 (Southern Dry Zone). Key districts: Tumakuru, Kolar, Chikkaballapur, Ramanagara, Hassan, Mandya, Bengaluru Rural, and Chitradurga.
• National Scope: Karnataka is the leading producer of Ragi in India (accounting for over 55% of national ragi production), followed by Uttarakhand, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Odisha, and Maharashtra.

Recommended Ragi (Finger Millet) Varieties:
• Rainfed / Dryland Varieties: GPU-28, GPU-48, KMR-301, ML-365, MR-6 (drought resistant), Indaf-8, GPU-67. Yield: 25–35 q/ha grain + 4–5 tonnes/ha valuable fodder.
• Irrigated Varieties: GPU-28, GPU-45, KMR-204, Indaf-9. Yield: 40–50 q/ha.

Sowing & Transplanting Period for Ragi (Finger Millet):
• Kharif Season: Optimum window is July 1 to August 15. Direct seeding rate: 5 kg/ha. Transplanted ragi nursery: 3 kg/ha (transplant 20-day old seedlings at 25 cm x 10 cm spacing).
• Early Kharif: June 15 to June 30 (for long duration varieties like Indaf-8).

Soil & Land Preparation for Ragi (Finger Millet):
• Soil Type: Well-drained red sandy loams, gravelly light red soils, and loamy soils with pH 5.5–7.5. Excellent drought tolerance; handles low fertility dryland soils.
• Land Prep: 2 ploughings to obtain friable seedbed. Incorporate 7.5 tonnes/ha FYM 3 weeks before sowing.

Fertilizer Recommendations for Ragi (Finger Millet) (NPK per Hectare):
• Rainfed Ragi (Dryland): 50 kg Nitrogen (N) : 40 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 25 kg Potassium (K2O).
  - Basal Application: Apply 50% Nitrogen + 100% Phosphorus + 100% Potassium at sowing.
  - Top-Dressing (30 Days After Sowing): Apply remaining 50% Nitrogen after weeding/thinning.
• Irrigated Ragi: 100 kg Nitrogen (N) : 50 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 50 kg Potassium (K2O) per hectare.
• Bio-Fertilizers: Seed treatment with Azospirillum @ 200 g/ha + Aspergillus awamori (PSB) @ 200 g/ha increases nutrient uptake.

Critical Irrigation & Growth Stages for Ragi (Finger Millet):
1. Germination & Seedling Phase: 0–15 Days After Sowing (DAS).
2. Tillering & Branching Stage: 30–35 DAS (critical period for inter-cultivation and weeding).
3. Earhead Emergence / Flowering Stage: 50–60 DAS (MOST CRITICAL for rainfed ragi — one protective irrigation during drought doubles yield).
4. Grain Filling & Dough Stage: 75–80 DAS.
5. Maturity & Harvest: 105–120 DAS (earheads turn brown and hard).

Pest & Disease Management for Ragi (Finger Millet):
• Ragi Blast (Pyricularia grisea — Leaf, Neck, Finger Blast): Spray Carbendazim 50 WP @ 1 g/litre or Mancozeb 75 WP @ 2 g/litre at earhead emergence.
• Ragi Stem Borer & Aphids: Spray Dimethoate 30 EC @ 1.7 ml/litre or Quinalphos 25 EC @ 2 ml/litre.
• Foot Rot / Wilt: Seed treatment with Trichoderma viride @ 4 g/kg seed before sowing.
