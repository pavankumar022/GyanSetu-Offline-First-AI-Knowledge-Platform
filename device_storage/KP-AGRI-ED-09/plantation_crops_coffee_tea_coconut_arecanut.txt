Plantation Crops Cultivation — Coffee, Tea, Coconut, Arecanut & Cashew
Source: ICAR-Central Plantation Crops Research Institute (CPCRI), Kasaragod; Central Coffee Research Institute (CCRI) & UPASI

Suitable Agro-Climatic Zones & Soil Requirements:
• Coffee: Karnataka (Kodagu, Chikkamagaluru, Hassan — accounts for >70% Indian coffee), Kerala (Wayanad), Tamil Nadu (Nilgiris). Rich organic-rich deep acidic soils (pH 5.5–6.5) under shade trees at 800–1600m altitude.
• Tea: Assam (Brahmaputra valley), West Bengal (Darjeeling GI, Dooars), Tamil Nadu (Nilgiris), Kerala (Munnar). Deep acidic well-drained loams with pH 4.5–5.5.
• Coconut: Kerala, Karnataka (Tumakuru, Hassan, Mandya, Coastal), Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh. Well-drained sandy loams, alluvial, and coastal red clay loams with pH 5.2–8.0.
• Arecanut (Betelnut): Karnataka (Shimoga, Chikkamagaluru, Dakshina Kannada — No. 1 state), Kerala, Assam. Deep well-drained clay loams with pH 5.5–6.5.
• Cashew: Maharashtra (Konkan), Andhra Pradesh, Odisha, Karnataka (Dakshina Kannada, Udupi), Goa. Well-drained red laterite soils with pH 5.0–6.5.

Recommended Varieties by Crop:
• Coffee: Arabica (Selection 795, Sln 9, Chandragiri — high cup quality); Robusta (Sln 274, CxR — high disease resistance). Yield: 1,000–1,500 kg clean coffee/ha.
• Tea: TV-1, TV-23 (Assam type); AV-2 (Darjeeling high flavor clone). Yield: 2,000–2,500 kg made tea/ha.
• Coconut: West Coast Tall (WCT), Chowghat Orange Dwarf (COD), Kalparaksha, Kera Sankara (Hybrid DXT). Yield: 80–120 nuts/palm/year.
• Arecanut: Mangala, Sumangala, Shreemangala, Kasaragod. Yield: 3.0–3.5 kg dry processed nut/palm/year.
• Cashew: Vengurla-4, Ullal-1, Bhaskara, Madakkathara-2. Yield: 10–15 kg raw nuts/tree/year.

Sowing Seasons & Planting Window:
• Coffee & Tea Planting: June to September (south-west monsoon onset).
• Coconut & Arecanut Planting: May–June (pre-monsoon) or September–October.
• Cashew Planting: June to August (graft plantlets).

Row Spacing & Plant Density:
• Coffee Spacing: Arabica 2.0 m x 2.0 m (2,500 plants/ha); Robusta 3.0 m x 3.0 m (1,111 plants/ha).
• Tea Spacing: Single hedge 1.0 m x 0.75 m (13,000 bushes/ha).
• Coconut Spacing: Square planting 7.5 m x 7.5 m (178 palms/ha). Pit size: 1 m x 1 m x 1 m.
• Arecanut Spacing: 2.7 m x 2.7 m (1,370 palms/ha).
• Cashew Spacing: Normal 7.5 m x 7.5 m (178 trees/ha); High Density 4.0 m x 4.0 m (625 trees/ha).

Irrigation Requirement & Water Management:
• Water Type: Drip / Sprinkler / Basin Irrigated.
• Irrigation Schedule:
  - Coffee: Blossom Irrigation (25 mm sprinkler water in March) is MOST CRITICAL to trigger uniform flowering.
  - Coconut: Drip 80–100 litres water/palm/day during dry summer months (doubles nut retention).
  - Arecanut: Drip 20–25 litres/palm/day during summer. Highly sensitive to drought.
  - Cashew: Protective drip irrigation @ 30 L/tree/day during flowering & fruit set (Jan–March).

Fertilizer & NPK Recommendations (per Tree / Palm / Hectare / Year):
• Coffee (Arabica Bearing): 140 kg N : 95 kg P2O5 : 140 kg K2O per hectare in 3 split doses.
• Coconut (Bearing Palm): 500 g N : 320 g P2O5 : 1,200 g K2O + 50 kg FYM per palm per year (apply in Sept & May).
• Arecanut (Bearing Palm): 100 g N : 40 g P2O5 : 140 g K2O + 12 kg green leaf manure per palm per year.
• Cashew (Bearing Tree): 500 g N : 125 g P2O5 : 125 g K2O per tree per year.

Growth Stages & Duration:
• Coffee: Blossom March; Berry Development April–October; Harvest Nov–Jan (Arabica) & Jan–March (Robusta).
• Tea: Plucking flush every 7–10 days throughout warm wet season.
• Coconut: Stabilizes yield at 7 years; bearing continues for 60+ years.
• Arecanut: Bearing starts at 5 years; harvest Oct–Dec.
• Cashew: Flowering Jan–Feb; Harvest March–May.

Common Pest Control (Insect Pests):
• Coffee White Stem Borer (Xylotrechus quadripes): Swab main stem with Lime-Neem paste or Chlorpyriphos 20 EC @ 6 ml/litre in April and October.
• Coconut Rhinoceros Beetle & Red Palm Weevil: Place Naphthalene balls in leaf axils; inject Imidacloprid 17.8 SL @ 2 ml/litre into trunk for Red Palm Weevil.
• Arecanut Spindle Bug: Application of Phorate 10 G @ 10 g/palm or spray Imidacloprid @ 0.3 ml/litre.
• Cashew Stem Borer & Tea Mosquito Bug: Spray Lambdacyhalothrin 5 EC @ 0.6 ml/litre at panicle emergence.

Common Disease Management (Fungal & Bacterial):
• Coffee Leaf Rust (Hemileia vastatrix): Spray Bordeaux mixture 0.5% or Hexaconazole 5 EC @ 1 ml/litre in May and September.
• Coconut Bud Rot & Root Wilt: Drench crown with Bordeaux mixture 1% or Copper Oxychloride @ 3 g/litre.
• Arecanut Koleroga / Fruit Rot (Phytophthora meadii — Major Disease): Spray 1% Bordeaux mixture before monsoon onset (May) and repeat 45 days later.
• Cashew Die-Back / Anthracnose: Spray Carbendazim 50 WP @ 1 g/litre.

Expected Yield:
• Coffee: 1.0–1.5 t/ha. Coconut: 80–120 nuts/palm/yr. Arecanut: 3.0–3.5 kg dry nut/palm/yr. Cashew: 10–15 kg/tree/yr.

Top Producing States:
• Coffee: Karnataka (Kodagu, Chikkamagaluru), Kerala. Tea: Assam, WB, TN. Coconut: KL, KA, TN. Arecanut: KA, KL. Cashew: MH, AP, KA.
