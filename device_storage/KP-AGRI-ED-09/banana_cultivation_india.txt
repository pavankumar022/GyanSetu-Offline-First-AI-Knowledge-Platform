Banana Cultivation Guidelines — National & Regional Advisory
Source: ICAR-National Research Centre for Banana (NRCB), Tiruchirappalli; IIHR Bengaluru & TNAU Coimbatore

Suitable Agro-Climatic Zones for Banana:
• Top Producing States: Andhra Pradesh (No. 1 banana producing state in India), Maharashtra (Jalgaon — "Banana City"), Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Karnataka (Mysuru, Nanjangud, Mandya, Shimoga), Uttar Pradesh, and Kerala.
• Soil & Climate: Well-drained rich clay loam or alluvial soils with pH 6.5–7.5 and high organic content. Requires warm tropical climate (26–35°C); highly sensitive to frost, wind damage, and waterlogging.

Recommended Banana Varieties:
• Grand Naine (G9 — Tissue Culture, export quality, uniform bunch): Most popular commercial variety across India. Bunch weight: 25–35 kg. Yield: 70–90 tonnes/ha.
• Regional Specialty Varieties:
  - Nanjangud Rasabale (GI Tagged specialty of Karnataka — aromatic dessert banana).
  - Robusta / Harichhal (Cavendish group), Elakki / Yelakki Bale (Ney Poovan — small aromatic sweet banana popular in Karnataka & Tamil Nadu), Red Banana, Nendran (Kerala chips banana), Poovan.

Planting Season, Spacing & Propagation for Banana:
• Planting Windows: June–July (Kharif monsoon) or October–November. Tissue culture plantlets or sword suckers (weighted 1.5–2.0 kg).
• Spacing & Pit Size:
  - Grand Naine (G9): 1.8 m x 1.5 m (spacing for 3,700 plants/ha) or high density planting 1.5 m x 1.5 m.
  - Yelakki / Robusta: 2.0 m x 2.0 m. Pit size: 60 cm x 60 cm x 60 cm filled with topsoil + 10 kg FYM + 250 g Neem cake.

Fertilizer Recommendations for Banana (NPK per Plant / per Hectare):
• NPK Dose per Plant: 200 g Nitrogen (N) : 50 g Phosphorus (P2O5) : 300 g Potassium (K2O).
  - Nitrogen: Apply N in 4 split doses (at 30, 60, 90, and 120 days after planting).
  - Phosphorus: Apply 100% P at planting.
  - Potassium: Apply K in 3 split doses (at 60, 120, and 180 days — shoot emergence/bunch formation stage).
  - Micronutrient Spray: Spray NRCB Banana Special / IIHR Banana Special @ 5 g/litre water at 3rd, 5th, and 7th months to boost bunch weight.

Critical Irrigation & Growth Stages for Banana:
1. Vegetative Growth Phase: 0–4 months (requires 25–30 litres water/plant/day).
2. Flower Inflorescence Initiation (Shooting): 7–9 months (MOST CRITICAL water requirement — 40 litres water/plant/day; avoid water deficit).
3. Bunch Development & Fruit Filling: 9–11 months (propping / staking with bamboo poles to prevent leaning under bunch weight).
4. Harvest: 11–13 months from planting (harvest when fingers turn plump and angles disappear).

Pest & Disease Management for Banana:
• Panama Wilt (Fusarium oxysporum f. sp. cubense — TR4 / Tropical Race 4): Drench soil with Carbendazim 50 WP @ 2 g/litre; use disease-free tissue culture G9 plantlets.
• Sigatoka Leaf Spot (Mycosphaerella musicola): Spray Propiconazole 25 EC @ 1 ml/litre + Mineral Oil @ 1% or Chlorothalonil @ 2 g/litre at initial leaf spot symptoms.
• Banana Pseudostem Borer & Rhizome Weevil: Application of Neem cake @ 250 g/plant; inject Chlorpyriphos 20 EC @ 2 ml/litre into pseudostem.
• Banana Bunchy Top Virus (BBTV): Eradicate vector banana aphid (Pentalonia nigronervosa) by spraying Imidacloprid 17.8 SL @ 0.3 ml/litre.
