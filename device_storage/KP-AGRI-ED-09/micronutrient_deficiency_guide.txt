Micronutrient Deficiency Identification & Soil Correction Guide
Source: ICAR-Indian Institute of Soil Science (IISS), Bhopal; All India Coordinated Research Project on Micro and Secondary Nutrients

1. Zinc (Zn) Deficiency Identification & Correction:
• Symptoms: Interveinal chlorosis (yellowing) on younger leaves; "White Bud" in Maize/Corn; "Khaira Disease" in Rice/Paddy (brown rusty leaf spots); stunted growth and shortened internodes ("Little Leaf" in Cotton/Citrus).
• Soil Correction Dose: Apply Zinc Sulfate (ZnSO4 - 21% Zn) @ 25 kg/ha as basal soil application before sowing.
• Foliar Correction Spray: Spray Zinc Sulfate @ 0.5% (5 g/litre) + Slaked Lime (Ca(OH)2) @ 0.25% (2.5 g/litre) at 30 and 45 DAS.

2. Boron (B) Deficiency Identification & Correction:
• Symptoms: Hollow heart in Sunflower & Groundnut; fruit cracking in Tomato & Pomegranate; death of terminal growing buds; poor pollen germination leading to hollow seed formation and low pod set in oilseeds/pulses.
• Soil Correction Dose: Apply Borax (10.5% B) @ 10–15 kg/ha or Solubor (20% B) @ 5 kg/ha as basal soil application.
• Foliar Correction Spray: Spray Solubor (Boron 20%) @ 0.2% (2 g/litre water) at ray floret stage in sunflower, or flowering stage in tomato/groundnut.

3. Iron (Fe) Deficiency Identification & Correction:
• Symptoms: Ivory-white to pale yellow chlorosis on young emerging leaves while leaf veins remain green ("Interveinal Iron Chlorosis"); common in calcareous alkaline high-pH soils (>8.0).
• Soil & Foliar Correction: Soil application of Ferrous Sulfate (FeSO4 - 19% Fe) @ 25 kg/ha mixed with FYM. Foliar spray of Ferrous Sulfate @ 1.0% (10 g/litre) + Citric Acid @ 1 g/litre at 15-day intervals.

4. Sulfur (S) Deficiency Identification & Correction:
• Symptoms: Pale yellow color on top young leaves (unlike Nitrogen deficiency which appears first on older lower leaves); low oil content in oilseeds (Mustard, Groundnut, Sunflower, Soybean).
• Soil Correction Dose: Apply Gypsum (CaSO4 - 18% S) @ 200–500 kg/ha or Elemental Sulfur (90% S) @ 25 kg/ha at land preparation.

5. Manganese (Mn) & Copper (Cu) Deficiency Identification & Correction:
• Manganese Symptoms: "Pahala Blight" in Sugarcane; grey specks on oat/wheat leaves in sandy soils of Punjab/Haryana. Spray Manganese Sulfate @ 0.5% (5 g/litre).
• Copper Symptoms: "Die-back" in Citrus; exanthema and chlorosis in wheat/barley. Apply Copper Sulfate (CuSO4) @ 10 kg/ha or foliar spray @ 0.2%.
