Tomato Cultivation Guidelines — National & Regional Advisory
Source: ICAR-Indian Institute of Horticultural Research (IIHR), Bengaluru; TNAU Coimbatore & IARI New Delhi

Suitable Agro-Climatic Zones for Tomato:
• Top Producing States: Andhra Pradesh (No. 1 tomato producer), Madhya Pradesh, Karnataka (Kolar — tomato hub of Karnataka, Chikkaballapur, Belagavi), Odisha, Gujarat, Maharashtra, and Tamil Nadu.
• Soil & Climate: Well-drained sandy loams to red loamy soils rich in organic matter with pH 6.0–7.0. Optimum growth temperature 21–24°C; sensitive to frost and waterlogging.

Recommended Tomato Varieties & Hybrids:
• IIHR Arka Varieties (TLCV & Bacterial Wilt Resistant): Arka Rakshak (triple disease resistant hybrid — TLCV, ToMV, Bacterial Wilt), Arka Samrat, Arka Vikas, Arka Abhed. Yield: 75–100 tonnes/ha.
• Commercial Hybrids: US-440, Syngenta Abhinav, Heemonso, Pusa Ruby, Pusa-120. Yield: 60–80 tonnes/ha.

Sowing, Nursery & Transplanting for Tomato:
• Planting Seasons: Autumn-Winter (sowing Sept–Oct); Spring-Summer (sowing Jan–Feb); Kharif (sowing June–July).
• Nursery & Seed Rate: Hybrid tomato seed rate: 100–125 g/ha. Open pollinated varieties: 250 g/ha. Transplant 25–30 day old nursery seedlings at 60 cm x 45 cm spacing (determinate) or 90 cm x 45 cm (indeterminate staked).

Fertilizer Recommendations for Tomato (NPK per Hectare):
• Irrigated Hybrid Tomato: 180 kg Nitrogen (N) : 120 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 150 kg Potassium (K2O).
  - Basal Application: 50% Nitrogen + 100% Phosphorus + 50% Potassium at transplanting.
  - 30 Days After Transplanting (DAT): 25% Nitrogen + 25% Potassium.
  - 45 DAT (Flowering & Fruit Set): 25% Nitrogen + 25% Potassium.
  - Calcium & Boron Foliar Spray: Spray Calcium Nitrate @ 5 g/litre + Boron @ 1 g/litre at fruit development to prevent Blossom End Rot (BER) and fruit cracking.

Critical Irrigation & Growth Stages for Tomato:
1. Nursery & Transplanting Phase: 0–25 days.
2. Vegetative Growth & Staking: 25–40 DAT (staking indeterminate varieties with eucalyptus poles/twine prevents soil rot).
3. Flowering & Fruit Set Stage: 45–60 DAT (MOST CRITICAL water requirement stage; uneven watering causes fruit cracking).
4. Fruit Development & Harvesting: 70–120 DAT (pick green mature for long distance transport; red ripe for local market).

Pest & Disease Management for Tomato:
• Tomato Pinworm (Tuta absoluta) & Fruit Borer (Helicoverpa armigera): Install Pheromone traps @ 8/acre. Spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 0.3 ml/litre or Spinotoram 11.7 SC @ 0.8 ml/litre.
• Tomato Leaf Curl Virus (TLCV — Whitefly Vector): Control whitefly vector by installing yellow sticky traps @ 20/acre; spray Cyantraniliprole 10.26 OD @ 1.8 ml/litre; grow resistant hybrid Arka Rakshak.
• Early Blight (Alternaria solani) & Late Blight: Spray Mancozeb 75 WP @ 2.5 g/litre or Azoxystrobin @ 1 ml/litre.
