Sugarcane Cultivation Guidelines — National & Karnataka Advisory
Source: ICAR-Sugarcane Breeding Institute (SBI), Coimbatore; ICAR-Indian Institute of Sugarcane Research (IISR), Lucknow

Suitable Agro-Climatic Zones for Sugarcane:
• Top Producing States: Uttar Pradesh (No. 1 area), Maharashtra (No. 1 sugar recovery), Karnataka (3rd largest producer), Tamil Nadu, Gujarat, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, and Bihar.
• Karnataka Sugarcane Belts: Belagavi (Sugarcane capital of Karnataka — Sankeshwar/Chikodi belt), Bagalkot, Vijayapura, Mandya (Visvesvaraya Canal command), Mysuru, Haveri, and Kalaburagi.

Recommended Sugarcane Varieties:
• Tropical South India (Karnataka, Maharashtra, Tamil Nadu):
  - Co-86032 (Nayana — top sucrose variety, high tonnage), CoC-671, Co-0238 (Subhash), Co-91010, SNK-09293 (drought tolerant for Northern dry zones), VSI-434. Yield: 100–140 tonnes/ha.
• Sub-Tropical North India (UP, Bihar, Punjab, Haryana):
  - Co-0238, Co-0118, Co-98014, CoLk-94184 (waterlogging tolerant). Yield: 80–110 tonnes/ha.

Planting Seasons & Planting Material for Sugarcane:
• Planting Seasons in South India:
  - Adsali Planting: July 15 to August 30 (15–18 months crop duration; highest yield 130–160 t/ha).
  - Pre-Seasonal Planting: October 15 to November 30 (14–15 months duration).
  - Suru / Spring Planting: January 15 to February 28 (11–12 months duration).
• Seed Sett Requirement: 40,000 two-budded setts or 75,000 single-budded setts per hectare. Treatment with Carbendazim 50 WP @ 1 g/litre water for 15 minutes before planting.

Soil & Land Preparation for Sugarcane:
• Soil Type: Deep clay loams, well-drained alluvial soils, and heavy black clay soils with pH 6.5–8.0.
• Land Prep: Deep subsoiling to 45 cm depth followed by 2 disk harrowings. Form ridges and furrows at 90 cm to 120 cm row spacing (wider 150 cm row spacing for drip irrigation).

Fertilizer Recommendations for Sugarcane (NPK per Hectare):
• Sugarcane Plant Crop (South India / Karnataka): 250 kg Nitrogen (N) : 75 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 75 kg K2O.
  - At Planting: 10% Nitrogen + 100% Phosphorus + 33% Potassium.
  - 45 Days After Planting (Tillering): 20% Nitrogen.
  - 90 Days After Planting: 30% Nitrogen.
  - Final Earthing Up (120–150 Days): 40% Nitrogen + 67% Potassium.
• Sugarcane Ratoon Crop: 300 kg Nitrogen (N) : 100 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 100 kg Potassium (K2O) + 25 kg Ferrous Sulfate / Zinc Sulfate per hectare.

Critical Irrigation & Growth Stages for Sugarcane:
1. Germination Phase: 0–45 Days After Planting (DAP).
2. Formative / Tillering Stage: 45–120 DAP (MOST CRITICAL for yield — water stress reduces tiller survival by 35%).
3. Grand Growth Period (Stem Elongation & Internode Formation): 120–270 DAP (highest biomass accumulation; requires regular waterings every 8–10 days).
4. Maturity & Sucrose Ripening: 270–360 DAP (stop irrigation 25–30 days prior to harvest to promote sugar synthesis).

Pest & Disease Management for Sugarcane:
• Early Shoot Borer (Chilo infuscatellus): Soil application of Chlorantraniliprole 0.4% GR @ 7.5 kg/acre at 30 DAP or spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 0.4 ml/litre at 45 DAP.
• Sugarcane Red Rot (Colletotrichum falcatum): Use disease-free certified setts; treat setts in hot water at 52°C for 30 minutes before planting. Destroy infected stools immediately.
• Woolly Aphid (Ceratovacuna lanigera): Release predators Dipha aphidivora or Chrysoperla carnea; spray Thiamethoxam 25 WG @ 0.2 g/litre.
