Horticulture Vegetable Crops Cultivation — Brinjal, Chilli, Okra, Cabbage & Cauliflower
Source: ICAR-Indian Institute of Horticultural Research (IIHR), Bengaluru; TNAU & IARI New Delhi

Suitable Agro-Climatic Zones & Soil Requirements:
• Brinjal & Chilli: Andhra Pradesh (Guntur chilli hub), Telangana, Karnataka (Belagavi, Haveri, Byadgi chilli GI belt), West Bengal, Odisha, Maharashtra. Well-drained fertile clay loams or red loamy soils rich in organic matter with pH 6.0–7.5.
• Okra (Bhindi): Gujarat, Maharashtra, AP, Karnataka, UP, Bihar. Sandy loams to clay loams with pH 6.5–7.5.
• Cabbage & Cauliflower (Cole Crops): West Bengal, Bihar, UP, Odisha, Punjab, Karnataka (Belagavi, Kolar winter belt). Deep rich friable sandy loams with pH 6.0–6.8. Sensitive to soil acidity and waterlogging.

Recommended Varieties & Hybrids:
• Brinjal (Eggplant): Arka Anand (bacterial wilt resistant hybrid), Pusa Purple Long, Utkal Anushree, Manjari. Yield: 40–50 tonnes/ha.
• Chilli: Byadgi Dabbi (GI Tag — high color, low pungency), Byadgi Kaddi, Arka Meghana, Arka Harita, US-700. Yield: 15–25 q/ha dry chilli.
• Okra (Bhindi): Arka Anamika (YVMV resistant), Pusa A-4, Mahyco 10, Samrat. Yield: 12–18 tonnes/ha.
• Cabbage: Golden Acre, Pusa Drumhead, Pride of India, BC-79 hybrid. Yield: 30–45 tonnes/ha.
• Cauliflower: Pusa Deepali (early), Pusa Synthetic (mid), Pusa Snowball K-1 (late). Yield: 20–35 tonnes/ha.

Sowing Seasons & Nursery Window:
• Kharif Season: Nursery May–June, Transplant June–July.
• Rabi Season: Nursery Sept–Oct, Transplant Oct–Nov.
• Summer (Zaid) Season: Nursery Jan–Feb, Transplant Feb–March.

Row Spacing & Seed Rate:
• Brinjal & Chilli Seed Rate: Seed rate: 200–250 g/ha (hybrids 100–125 g/ha). Transplant 30-day seedlings at 60 cm x 45 cm spacing (Chilli) or 75 cm x 60 cm (Brinjal).
• Okra (Bhindi) Seed Rate: Kharif: 8–10 kg/ha; Summer: 12–15 kg/ha (45 cm x 30 cm spacing). Direct line sowing.
• Cabbage & Cauliflower Seed Rate: 400–500 g/ha (hybrids 250 g/ha). Transplant 25-day seedlings at 45 cm x 45 cm spacing.

Irrigation Requirement & Water Management:
• Water Type: Drip / Furrow Irrigated.
• Critical Growth Stages needing Irrigation:
  - Chilli / Brinjal: Irrigate every 7–8 days. Critical at Flowering and Fruit Development stages. Water stress causes flower and fruit drop.
  - Okra: Requires watering every 4–5 days in summer; 8–10 days in Kharif.
  - Cabbage / Cauliflower: Requires uniform soil moisture throughout head/curd formation (every 5–7 days). Uneven watering causes head cracking or riceyness in curd.

Fertilizer & NPK Recommendations (per Hectare):
• Brinjal & Chilli Dose: 150 kg Nitrogen (N) : 75 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 75 kg Potassium (K2O). Split N in 3 doses (basal, 30 DAT, 50 DAT).
• Okra Dose: 100 kg Nitrogen (N) : 50 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 50 kg Potassium (K2O).
• Cabbage & Cauliflower Dose: 150 kg Nitrogen (N) : 80 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 100 kg Potassium (K2O) + 15 kg Borax (prevents Hollow Stem in Cauliflower).

Growth Stages & Duration:
• Brinjal: First picking at 60–70 DAT; harvesting continues for 4–5 months.
• Chilli: Green chilli harvesting at 60 DAT; Dry red chilli picking at 110–130 DAT.
• Okra: First picking 45–50 DAS (pick every alternate day for tender pods).
• Cabbage & Cauliflower: Head/Curd maturity 65–90 DAT depending on early/late variety.

Common Pest Control (Insect Pests):
• Brinjal Shoot & Fruit Borer (Leucinodes orbonalis — Major Pest): Clip infected shoots weekly; spray Emamectin Benzoate 5 SG @ 0.4 g/litre or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 0.3 ml/litre.
• Chilli Thrips (Scirtothrips dorsalis) & Mites: Spray Fipronil 5 SC @ 1.5 ml/litre or Spinetoram 11.7 SC @ 0.8 ml/litre; apply Blue Sticky Traps @ 15/acre.
• Okra Fruit Borer & Jassids: Spray Flubendiamide 39.35 SC @ 0.3 ml/litre.
• Cabbage Diamondback Moth (DBM — Plutella xylostella): Spray Bacillus thuringiensis (Bt) @ 2 g/litre or Spinotoram @ 0.8 ml/litre.

Common Disease Management (Fungal & Viral):
• Chilli Leaf Curl Virus (Gemini Virus — Whitefly Vector): Install Yellow Sticky Traps @ 20/acre; spray Cyantraniliprole 10.26 OD @ 1.8 ml/litre.
• Brinjal Phomopsis Blight & Fruit Rot: Spray Mancozeb 75 WP @ 2.5 g/litre or Carbendazim @ 1 g/litre.
• Okra Yellow Vein Mosaic Virus (YVMV): Grow YVMV-resistant varieties (Arka Anamika); rogue out infected yellow-veined plants early.
• Cauliflower Clubroot & Black Rot (Xanthomonas): Soil drenching with Copper Oxychloride @ 3 g/litre + Streptocycline @ 0.5 g/litre; apply Lime to acidic soils.

Expected Yield:
• Brinjal: 40–50 t/ha. Chilli: 15–25 q/ha dry. Okra: 12–18 t/ha. Cabbage: 30–45 t/ha. Cauliflower: 20–35 t/ha.

Top Producing States:
• Chilli: Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka. Brinjal: West Bengal, Odisha. Okra: Gujarat, Maharashtra. Cabbage/Cauliflower: WB, Bihar, UP.
