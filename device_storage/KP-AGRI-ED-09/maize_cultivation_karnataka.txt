Maize (Corn) Cultivation Guidelines — Karnataka & All-India Advisory
Source: ICAR-Indian Institute of Maize Research (IIMR), Ludhiana; University of Agricultural Sciences (UAS), Dharwad & Bengaluru

Suitable Agro-Climatic Zones for Maize (Corn):
• Karnataka Zones: Zone 3 (Northern Dry Zone), Zone 8 (Northern Transition Zone), Zone 4 (Central Dry Zone), and Zone 5 (Eastern Dry Zone). Key districts: Davanagere (Maize bowl of Karnataka), Haveri, Belagavi, Chamarajanagar, Shimoga, Chitradurga, and Bellary.
• National Scope: Karnataka is the No. 1 maize-producing state in India, followed by Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Bihar, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, and Rajasthan.

Recommended Maize (Corn) Hybrids & Varieties:
• Irrigated Hybrids: MAH-14-138, MAH-14-5, Nithyashree (NAH-1137), Deccan-103, CP-818, Pioneer 30V92, Bio 9681, NK-6240. Yield: 65–85 q/ha.
• Rainfed / Dryland Hybrids: MAH-14-138, Amber Popcorn, Sweet Corn hybrid Priya, Bio-9637. Yield: 45–60 q/ha.

Sowing Period & Seasons for Maize (Corn):
• Kharif (Monsoon): Best sowing window is June 15 to July 15 (with onset of monsoon rains). Seed rate: 20 kg/ha for hybrid maize, 8 kg/ha for sweet corn.
• Rabi (Irrigated Winter): October 15 to November 30.
• Spring / Summer (Irrigated): January 15 to February 28.

Soil & Land Preparation for Maize (Corn):
• Soil Type: Well-drained fertile red loamy soils, medium to deep black cotton soils (Vertisols) with pH 6.0–7.5.
• Land Prep: Deep ploughing followed by 2 cross-harrowings to obtain fine tilth. Construct ridges and furrows at 60 cm spacing. Sensitive to waterlogging — establish 15 cm deep drainage furrows every 4 rows.

Fertilizer Recommendations for Maize (Corn) (NPK per Hectare):
• Irrigated Hybrid Maize: 150 kg Nitrogen (N) : 75 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 40 kg Potassium (K2O).
  - Basal Dose: Apply 25% Nitrogen + full Phosphorus + full Potassium at time of sowing.
  - Knee-High Stage Top-Dressing (25–30 Days): Apply 50% Nitrogen.
  - Tasseling Stage Top-Dressing (45–50 Days): Apply remaining 25% Nitrogen.
  - Micronutrients: Apply Zinc Sulfate @ 25 kg/ha as basal application to prevent White Bud deficiency in maize.
• Rainfed Maize (Corn): 100 kg Nitrogen (N) : 50 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 25 kg Potassium (K2O) per hectare.

Critical Irrigation & Growth Stages for Maize (Corn):
1. Germination & Seedling Establishment: 0–15 Days After Sowing (DAS).
2. Knee-High Stage: 25–30 DAS (critical for first nitrogen top-dressing).
3. Tasseling Stage (Male Flowering): 45–50 DAS (MOST CRITICAL for moisture — water stress at tasseling reduces yield up to 40%).
4. Silking Stage (Female Flowering): 55–60 DAS.
5. Grain Dough & Filling Stage: 75–80 DAS (maintain moist soil).
6. Physiological Maturity & Harvest: 100–115 DAS (husks turn dry straw yellow).

Pest & Disease Management for Maize (Corn):
• Fall Armyworm (FAW - Spodoptera frugiperda): Spray Emamectin benzoate 5 SG @ 0.4 g/litre or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5 SC @ 0.4 ml/litre into leaf whorls at knee-high stage (20–25 DAS). Repeat after 15 days if whorl damage exceeds 10%.
• Turcicum Leaf Blight: Spray Mancozeb 75 WP @ 2.5 g/litre or Azoxystrobin 23 SC @ 1 ml/litre at initial leaf spot symptoms.
• Maize Stem Borer (Chilo partellus): Apply Carbofuran 3G granules @ 3 kg/acre inside leaf whorls at 20 DAS.
