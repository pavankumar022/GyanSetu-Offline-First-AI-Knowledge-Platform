Kisan Credit Card (KCC) Scheme — Agricultural Credit & Interest Subvention
Source: Reserve Bank of India (RBI); NABARD & Ministry of Agriculture

Scheme Purpose & Credit Limits:
• KCC provides timely, hassle-free credit to farmers for crop cultivation inputs (seeds, fertilizers, pesticides), post-harvest expenses, family consumption needs, maintenance of farm assets, and working capital for animal husbandry & fisheries.
• All-in-One Credit Limit: Fixes 5-year credit limit based on crop area, Scale of Finance (SOF), and 10% annual limit enhancement.

Interest Rates & Interest Subvention Scheme (ISS):
• Benchmark Interest Rate: 7.0% per annum for short-term crop loans up to ₹3.0 Lakh.
• Prompt Repayment Incentive (PRI): Government provides an additional 3.0% interest subvention for prompt repayment on or before due date.
• Effective Net Interest Rate: Farmers paying on time get loans at an effective net interest rate of just 4.0% per annum!
• Collateral-Free Credit Limit: No collateral security required for KCC crop loans up to ₹1.60 Lakh (enhanced to ₹3.0 Lakh for tie-up loans).

Eligibility Criteria for KCC:
• Individual Farmers / Owner Cultivators.
• Tenant Farmers, Sharecroppers, and Oral Lessees.
• Self Help Groups (SHGs) or Joint Liability Groups (JLGs) of farmers.
• Animal Husbandry & Dairy Farmers (up to ₹2.0 Lakh credit limit at 4% interest rate).
• Fisheries & Aquaculture Farmers (up to ₹2.0 Lakh working capital limit).

KCC Card Features & Benefits:
• Revolving Cash Credit Facility: Operates as an ATM-cum-Debit Card (RuPay Kisan Card) for cash withdrawals at ATMs and merchant POS terminals.
• Personal Accident Insurance: Accidental death / permanent disability cover up to ₹50,000; partial disability cover up to ₹25,000.
• No Processing Fee: Loan processing fee waived for KCC limits up to ₹3.0 Lakh.

How to Apply for KCC & Required Documents:
• Application Channels: Commercial Banks, Regional Rural Banks (RRBs like Karnataka Vikas Grameena Bank / Kaveri Grameena Bank), Cooperative Banks, or via PM-KISAN portal (Simplified 1-page KCC application form for PM-KISAN beneficiaries).
• Required Documents: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card / Voter ID, Land Record (Pahani / RTC / Khata extract), 2 Passport size photographs, Crop Sowing Declaration.
