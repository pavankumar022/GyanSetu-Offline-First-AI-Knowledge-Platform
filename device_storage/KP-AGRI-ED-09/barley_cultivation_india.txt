Barley Cultivation Guidelines — National & Regional Advisory
Source: ICAR-Indian Institute of Wheat and Barley Research (IIWBR), Karnal; Directorate of Barley Development

Suitable Agro-Climatic Zones for Barley:
• Top Producing States: Rajasthan (No. 1 barley producing state in India), Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, Haryana, Punjab, Himachal Pradesh, and Uttarakhand.
• Soil & Climate: Well-drained loam to clay loam soils with pH 6.5–8.0. Highly drought-tolerant and alkali/salt-tolerant cereal crop compared to wheat.

Recommended Barley Varieties:
• Malt Barley Varieties (Brewing Industry): DWRB-101, DWRB-182, DWRB-92, BCU-73. Yield: 45–55 q/ha.
• Feed & Dual-Purpose Varieties (Grain + Fodder): RD-2035, RD-2552, RD-2715, HUB-113, BCO-56. Yield: 40–50 q/ha.

Sowing Seasons & Seed Rate for Barley:
• Rabi Season: Optimum sowing window October 25 to November 15. Seed rate: 100 kg/ha for rainfed/irrigated grain crop; 120 kg/ha for dual-purpose fodder crop (22.5 cm row spacing).

Fertilizer Recommendations for Barley (NPK per Hectare):
• Irrigated Malt Barley: 60 kg Nitrogen (N) : 30 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 20 kg Potassium (K2O). Apply 50% N + full P & K at sowing; top dress 50% N at first irrigation (25–30 DAS). Avoid excess N which increases grain protein above malt limits (>12%).
• Rainfed Barley: 40 kg Nitrogen (N) : 20 kg Phosphorus (P2O5) : 20 kg Potassium (K2O) as full basal dose at sowing.

Critical Irrigation & Growth Stages for Barley:
1. Germination & Seedling Phase: 0–15 Days After Sowing (DAS).
2. Crown Root Initiation (CRI): 25–30 DAS (most critical watering stage).
3. Tillering & Jointing Stage: 45–55 DAS.
4. Flowering & Heading Stage: 70–75 DAS.
5. Grain Milking & Maturity: 90–105 DAS (harvest when moisture drops below 12%).

Pest & Disease Management for Barley:
• Yellow Rust & Stripe Rust: Spray Propiconazole 25 EC @ 1 ml/litre water at initial symptom appearance.
• Barley Aphids: Spray Thiamethoxam 25 WG @ 0.2 g/litre or Imidacloprid 17.8 SL @ 0.3 ml/litre.
• Covered Smut: Seed treatment with Carboxin 75 WP @ 2.5 g/kg seed before sowing.
