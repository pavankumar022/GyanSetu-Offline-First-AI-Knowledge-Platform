Integrated Pest Management (IPM) Guidelines & Economic Threshold Levels
Source: ICAR-National Centre for Integrated Pest Management (NCIPM); Directorate of Plant Protection, Quarantine & Storage (DPPQ&S)

Principles & Hierarchy of IPM:
• IPM integrates Cultural, Mechanical, Biological, and Chemical control methods to keep pest populations below the Economic Threshold Level (ETL), minimizing pesticide residues, cost, and environmental harm.

Economic Threshold Levels (ETL) for Major Crop Pests:
1. Rice / Paddy:
   - Brown Plant Hopper (BPH): 10 BPH/hill at tillering stage; 20 BPH/hill at panicle stage.
   - Yellow Stem Borer: 5% dead hearts at tillering or 1 egg mass/m2.
   - Paddy Leaf Folder: 2 damaged leaves/hill at tillering.
2. Cotton:
   - Pink Bollworm: 8 adult moths/trap/night for 3 consecutive nights or 10% green boll damage.
   - Whitefly: 6–8 nymphs or adults per leaf.
   - Jassids: 2 nymphs/leaf.
3. Maize / Corn:
   - Fall Armyworm (FAW): 10% whorl damage at seedling/knee-high stage; 20% damage at tasseling.
4. Chickpea / Tur (Gram Pod Borer):
   - Helicoverpa armigera: 1 larva per meter row length or 5-8% pod damage.

Cultural & Mechanical IPM Practices:
• Deep Summer Ploughing: Exposes soil-inhabiting pupae, cutworms, and root-knot nematodes to extreme solar heat and predator birds.
• Crop Rotation: Rotate host crops with non-host crops (e.g. rice followed by pulses or oilseeds) to break pest life cycles.
• Trap Cropping: Plant Marigold around Tomato/Cotton (attracts Helicoverpa egg laying); plant Mustard around Cabbage/Cauliflower (attracts diamondback moth).
• Synchronous Sowing: Community-wide sowing within a 10-day window avoids pest buildup on early/late sown fields.

Judicious Chemical Insecticide Use (Last Resort):
• Always select Green Triangle / Blue Triangle selective insecticides over broad-spectrum red label chemicals.
• Spraying Etiquette: Spray early morning (7–10 AM) or late evening (4–6 PM) when honeybee pollinators are inactive. Wear protective mask, gloves, and apron.
