Common Rural Disease Symptoms & PHC Referral Protocols
Source: National Health Mission (NHM); Integrated Disease Surveillance Programme (IDSP) & NVBDCP

1. Malaria (Plasmodium vivax & Plasmodium falciparum):
• Symptoms: High fever with sudden shivering chills, rigors, profuse sweating, severe headache, muscle pain, vomiting.
• Diagnostic Test: Rapid Diagnostic Test (RDT) kit or peripheral blood smear slide at Sub-Centre / PHC.
• Treatment / Referral: P. vivax: Chloroquine + Primaquine for 14 days. P. falciparum: Artemisinin-based Combination Therapy (ACT) for 3 days. Immediate PHC referral if altered sensorium, jaundice, or dark urine (Blackwater fever).

2. Dengue & Chikungunya Vector-Borne Fevers:
• Symptoms: Sudden high fever, retro-orbital pain (pain behind eyes), severe joint/muscle body break pain ("breakbone fever"), red petechial skin rashes.
• Red-Flag Symptoms for Emergency PHC Referral: Abdominal pain, persistent vomiting, mucosal bleeding (bleeding gums/nose), sudden drop in blood pressure (Dengue Shock Syndrome).

3. Tuberculosis (TB — Nikshay Programme):
• Screening Symptoms: Persistent cough for >2 weeks, low-grade evening fever, night sweats, unexplained weight loss, hemoptysis (blood in sputum).
• Action: Collect 2 sputum samples (spot + early morning) for CBNAAT / GeneXpert testing at PHC. Direct Benefit Transfer ₹500/month under Nikshay Poshan Yojana during DOTS treatment.

4. Typhoid Fever (Enteric Fever):
• Symptoms: Step-ladder pattern rising fever, abdominal discomfort, coated tongue, bradycardia, rose spots on chest.
• Action: Refer to PHC for Widal test / Blood culture and 10-14 day antibiotic course (Cefixime / Azithromycin).

5. Acute Respiratory Infection (ARI) & Pneumonia in Children:
• Screening Signs: Fast breathing (RR > 50/min in 2-12 months; RR > 40/min in 1-5 years), chest indrawing (stridor), grunting, inability to feed.
• Action: Immediate PHC referral for Amoxicillin syrup and oxygen support.
