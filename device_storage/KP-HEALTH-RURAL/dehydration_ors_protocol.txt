Dehydration Assessment & WHO ORS / Zinc Supplementation Protocol
Source: World Health Organization (WHO); Indian Academy of Pediatrics (IAP) & MoHFW Clinical Guidelines

Dehydration Severity Assessment Guidelines:
1. No Dehydration: Well, alert child; normal skin pinch (retracts immediately); normal tears, moist mouth.
2. Some Dehydration: Restless, irritable child; sunken eyes; dry mouth/tongue; skin pinch goes back slowly (<2 seconds); thirsty/drinks eagerly.
3. Severe Dehydration (Medical Emergency): Lethargic, unconscious or floppy child; sunken eyes; dry mouth; skin pinch goes back VERY slowly (>2 seconds); unable to drink/drinks poorly.

WHO Oral Rehydration Solution (ORS) Preparation & Dosage:
• Preparation Formula: Dissolve 1 full packet of WHO Reduced Osmolarity ORS (20.5 g) in 1 Litre of clean boiled cooled drinking water. Stir until fully dissolved.
• Crucial Rule: Use prepared ORS solution within 24 hours (discard leftover solution after 24 hours to prevent bacterial contamination).

ORS Administration Guidelines:
• Age Under 2 Years: 50–100 ml ORS solution after each loose watery stool (approx. 500 ml per day).
• Age 2 to 10 Years: 100–200 ml ORS solution after each loose watery stool (approx. 1 Litre per day).
• Age Above 10 Years & Adults: Drink as much ORS as wanted after each loose stool (2–3 Litres per day).
• Administration Technique: Give small frequent sips using cup and spoon. If child vomits, wait 10 minutes and resume ORS more slowly (spoonful every 2–3 minutes).

Zinc Supplementation Protocol for Acute Diarrhea:
• Duration: Administer Zinc Dispersible Tablet for 14 FULL DAYS continuously, even after diarrhea stops (reduces duration/severity and prevents recurrent diarrhea episodes for 3 months).
• Dosage:
  - Infants 2 to 6 Months: 10 mg Zinc daily (half tablet dissolved in 5 ml breastmilk/ORS) for 14 days.
  - Children 6 Months to 5 Years: 20 mg Zinc daily (1 full tablet dissolved in clean water/breastmilk) for 14 days.

Home-Made Fluid Alternatives (If WHO ORS Unavailable):
• Salt-Sugar Solution: 6 level teaspoons of sugar + 1/2 level teaspoon of salt dissolved in 1 Litre of clean drinking water.
• Rice Water (Kanji) with salt, Tender Coconut Water, Buttermilk (Majjige) with salt, or Dal soup.
• Danger Warning: NEVER give sugary soft drinks, commercial juices, or plain boiled water without electrolytes (worsens hyponatremia and osmotic diarrhea).
