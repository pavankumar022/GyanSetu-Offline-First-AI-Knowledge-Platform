MGNREGS Scheme — Job Card, Application Process, State Wage Rates & Grievance SOP
Source: Ministry of Rural Development (MoRD), Government of India; Mahatma Gandhi NREGA Act 2005

Statutory Entitlement & Guarantee:
• MGNREGS guarantees at least 100 days of wage employment in a financial year to every rural household whose adult members volunteer to do unskilled manual work.
• Equal Wages: Women and men are legally entitled to equal wages for equal work.

Job Card & Work Application Procedure:
• Job Card Application: Submit application to Gram Panchayat Secretary with Aadhaar, Bank Account details, and passport photograph. Gram Panchayat issues Job Card within 15 days free of cost.
• Work Application: Submit written application for work to Gram Panchayat stating date and duration. Dated receipt must be given to applicant.
• Work Allocation: Work must be provided within 15 days of application, within a 5 km radius of applicant's village. If work is provided beyond 5 km, an additional 10% transport/living allowance is paid.

Unemployment Allowance Guarantee (Section 7):
• If the Gram Panchayat / Block Officer fails to provide employment within 15 days of application, the state government is legally bound to pay a daily Unemployment Allowance:
  - First 30 Days: 25% of the notified state minimum wage.
  - Beyond 30 Days: 50% of the notified state minimum wage until work is provided.

Notified State MGNREGS Daily Wage Rates (FY 2024–25):
• Haryana: ₹374/day (highest wage rate in India).
• Karnataka: ₹349/day (enhanced under state notification).
• Punjab: ₹322/day.
• Rajasthan: ₹275/day.
• Uttar Pradesh / Bihar: ₹237/day.
• Madhya Pradesh: ₹243/day.

Worksite Facilities & Grievance Redressal:
• Mandatory Worksite Facilities: Safe drinking water, shade for children, first-aid box, and crèche (if >5 mothers are working).
• MGNREGA Ombudsman & Toll-Free Helpline: File complaint with District MGNREGA Ombudsman for delayed wage payment (>15 days delay attracts 0.05%/day interest compensation). Toll-free national helpline: 1800-11-1555.
